## After Launch

Once the program is live and consumers can start making referrals, the Extole CSM will schedule a meeting with the client to resolve any open launch items and ensure they have the tools needed to successfully manage and grow their program. Throughout this monitoring phase, the Extole CSM will also stay on as the primary point of contact to ensure that the program runs smoothly and that initial reward payouts go out, which is typically 60–90 days post-launch.

### Meeting Agenda

* Overview of Extole & BHN integrated services model
* Launch/Onboarding Handoff 
  * Capture any open times from launch
  * Confirm and schedule reports (BHN CDR to also be added to any scheduled performance reports)
  * Schedule a Consumer Support training with the Clients CSR team
* Schedule a recurring call with the client

### Meeting Attendees

| BHN                               | Extole                   | Client          |
| :-------------------------------- | :----------------------- | :-------------- |
| Onboarding Manager                | Implementation Manager   | Project Manager |
| Account Manager                   | Customer Success Manager | Program Manager |
| Client Development Representative |                          |                 |

<br />

## Transition to Integrated Services Model

Once Extole and BHN confirm that the initial reward payouts have been delivered to recipients (approximately 60–90 days post-launch), the BHN AM will take the lead on managing the client relationship. However, the Extole CSM will remain actively involved, providing support and guidance throughout the process.

## Overview

| Task                                                      | Description                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| :-------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Recurring meetings                                        | The BHN AM will lead recurring meetings to discuss status on projects and tickets. The Extole CSM will participate in recurring meetings and will collaborate with the BHN AM on meeting agendas. |
| Responding to client inquiries, requests, and escalations | The BHN AM will respond to and project manage inquiries with the client, and then engage the Extole Customer Success and Support teams for resolution.                                            |
| Program growth and expansion strategy                     | The BHN CDR and BHN CSM will collaborate on identifying and presenting opportunities to grow and expand the program using Extole provided playbooks.                                              |
| Implementation                                            | The Extole CSM and Extole SE will implement all program, campaign, and platform changes using the Extole administrative dashboard.                                                                |

# How your Customer Success Manager can help

### Reporting

* Recommend, configure, and schedule reports to understand program performance, participants, and rewards

### Program growth and expansion strategy

* Provide recommendations using best practices, anonymized benchmarks, and examples from thousands of Extole-powered campaigns

### Campaign and program management

* Add a new promotion link for an existing program
* Update images, copy, or links in an existing campaign
* Run an A/B test or a Burst (limited-time) campaign

### Launching a new program

* Each program has a unique customer journey (think `Refer a Consumer` vs a `Refer a Business`) and generally requires a new SOW and an assigned launch  (Extole) and onboarding (BHN) team. CSM's should loop in BHN and Extole sales for discovery & proposal.

# How to Collaborate with Extole Support

Extole uses a [HELP ticketing system](https://dev.extole.com/docs/contact-extole-help) for technical support. Any HELP requests from channel partners will be automatically entered into a specific queue based on channel partner status and prioritized accordingly. Additionally, clients who have US-only support included in their SOW will be routed to a US-based support engineer. 

> ❗️ First, sign-up for [Extole HELP](https://extole.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/3/user/login?destination=portal%2F3)
>
> Sign up for Extole's HELP ticketing system to view, manage, and receive real-time notifications for any submitted tickets.

To submit a support request, reach out to [support@extole.com](mailto:support@extole.com) and include:

* `Client name`
* A description of the issue
* The`email` or `user ID` of an end-consumer who is experiencing the issue
* Any available screenshots
* Any available [API error responses](🔗) 

## When to reach out to Extole support

### To escalate a "Where is My Reward?" (WISMR) request

* For the quickest resolution, **clients** should reach out to [support@extole.com](mailto:support@extole.com) if they need assistance with understanding why a consumer did not receive a reward. 
* If a card has already been delivered to a consumer and needs to be re-sent, clients can refer consumers to BHN card support.

### If consumers are not able to access or participate in the program

* BHN should immediately reach out to [support@extole.com](mailto:support@extole.com) if a client reports issues with consumers not being able to access program experiences or are not receiving emails and/or rewards. 
* The Extole Support Engineer (SE) will notify the BHN AM directly if Extole discovers any issues that impact end-consumers. 

### For any general technical inquiries or technical requests

* Add or troubleshoot an integration (API, SDK, File, Webhook)
* Add a consumer to audience
* Design custom emails or experiences
* Change campaign rules
* Manually cancel approve, or re-process rewards
