# Overview

A new Extole account is ready to use as soon as it's created. Until you set it up, your program uses Extole's defaults: a placeholder company name and colors, share links on an Extole domain, and emails from an Extole address.

Before your first program goes live, set up:

- Your brand
- Your program domain and email sending domain
- Integrations: site tags, events, and the platforms you already use
- Your team members
- Your first program, with its rewards, rules, and terms
- Testing, then go-live

Start with your brand. It takes a few minutes, and from then on My Extole shows your company's look instead of a placeholder. Ask your DNS team for the two domains early. They take a few days to complete, and nothing else waits for them, because your program works on Extole's domain and email address until they're done.

In My Extole, [Extole Chat](/product/extole-chat/index) can help. Ask it "Set up this account for Acme", using your company's name or website, and it adds your brand to the account. Ask "How's my setup?" to see what's done and what's left.

If you work with an Extole launch team, they'll take you through these in the order of your launch plan.

## Brand

Your brand is your company name, website, colors, fonts, logo, and icon. You set them once for the whole account, and every program and campaign uses them. See [My Account Settings](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/campaign-creation-and-editing/my-account-settings).

## Program Domain

Your share links and promotion links use a web address. A new account uses an Extole address, such as `mycompany.extole.io`. You can switch to an address on your own domain, such as `refer.mycompany.com`.

To switch, your DNS team adds one record. Extole provides the security certificate for the address. Your links keep working on the Extole address while you wait. See [Extole DNS Requirements](/technical/operational-tasks/account-configuration/extole-dns-requirements).

## Email Sending Domain

A new account sends program emails, such as reward emails, from an Extole address at `email.referral-mail.com`. The emails show your company name, but the address is Extole's. You can send them from an address on your own domain instead.

To switch, Extole gives you three records. Your DNS team adds them and updates one more. Then you set the address your emails come from. Your emails keep sending from the Extole address until the switch is complete. See [Extole DNS Requirements](/technical/operational-tasks/account-configuration/extole-dns-requirements) for the records, and [My Account Settings](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/campaign-creation-and-editing/my-account-settings) for the address.

## Integrations

Integrations connect your program to your website and your systems:

- **Site tags**: Extole's JavaScript tags on your website show your program's promotions and share experience, and record what customers do there.
- **Events**: your systems send Extole the events your program rewards, such as a purchase or a new account, through the API, a file upload, or a partner integration.
- **Your platforms**: an integration with a platform you already use, such as your ecommerce store, CRM, or email platform, sends Extole events and customers, or receives your program's data.

See [Integrating with Extole](/technical/integration-overview/integrating-with-extole). The **Partners** section of Technical Docs lists the platforms Extole integrates with.

## Team Members

Invite the people who will run the program so each has their own login. See [Inviting Team Members](/guides/user-management/managing-users/inviting-team-members).

## Your First Program

A program is one referral use case: who can take part and what earns a reward. You create a program from a template. See [Creating a Program](/guides/platform-overview/creating-a-program).

Three parts of the program are set up with it:

- **Rewards**: what your program gives customers, such as a gift card, a coupon, or an account credit. See [Rewards Overview and FAQ](/guides/rewards-management/setting-up-your-rewards/rewards-overview-and-faq).
- **Quality and reward rules**: which events count, and when a reward is given. See [Rules & Quality](/product/product-overview/rewards/rules-quality).
- **Terms and conditions**: the program's terms, reviewed by your legal team. See [How to Upload Terms & Conditions](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/extole-experiences/how-to-upload-terms-conditions).

## Testing and Go-Live

Before the program goes live, test it the way your customers will use it, from the promotion to the reward. For a referral program, test both sides: the customer who shares, and the friend who uses their link. See the [Go-Live QA Checklist](/guides/platform-overview/implementing-your-referral-program/go-live-qa-checklist).
