# Overview

A new Extole account is ready to use as soon as it's created. Until you set it up, your program uses Extole's defaults: a placeholder company name and colors, share links on an Extole domain, and emails from an Extole address.

Set up these six things before your first program goes live, in this order:

1. Brand
2. Program domain
3. Email sending domain
4. Integrations
5. Your first program
6. Rewards

The order matters because later steps use earlier ones. A program takes its look from your brand and uses your domains in its links and emails. A program also has to exist before you can give it a reward.

In My Extole, [Extole Chat](/product/extole-chat/index) can help. Ask it "Set up this account for Acme", using your company's name or website, and it adds your brand to the account. Ask "How's my setup?" to see what's done and what's left.

## Brand

Your brand is your company name, website, colors, fonts, logo, and icon. You set them once for the whole account, and every program and campaign uses them. See [My Account Settings](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/my-account-settings).

## Program Domain

Your share links and promotion links use a web address. A new account uses an Extole address, such as `mycompany.extole.io`. You can switch to an address on your own domain, such as `refer.mycompany.com`.

To switch, your DNS team adds one record. Extole provides the security certificate for the address. Your links keep working on the Extole address while you wait. See [Extole DNS Requirements](/technical/operational-tasks/account-configuration/extole-dns-requirements).

## Email Sending Domain

A new account sends program emails, such as reward emails, from an Extole address at `email.referral-mail.com`. The emails show your company name, but the address is Extole's. You can send them from an address on your own domain instead.

To switch, your DNS team adds three records and updates one more. Then you set the address your emails come from. Your emails keep sending from the Extole address until the switch is complete. See [Extole DNS Requirements](/technical/operational-tasks/account-configuration/extole-dns-requirements) for the records, and [My Account Settings](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/my-account-settings) for the address.

## Integrations

An integration connects Extole to a platform you already use, such as your ecommerce store or CRM. It sends Extole the events and customers your program needs. The **Partners** section of Technical Docs lists the platforms Extole integrates with.

## Your First Program

A program is one referral use case: who can take part and what earns a reward. You create a program from a template. See [Creating a Program](/guides/platform-overview/creating-a-program).

## Rewards

A reward is what your program gives people, such as a gift card, a coupon, or an account credit. Add the reward after you create the program. See [Rewards Overview and FAQ](/guides/rewards-management/setting-up-your-rewards/rewards-overview-and-faq).
