# Overview

A new Extole account works from its first day on Extole's own domain and email address. Setting up the parts below makes your program look, link, and send like your company, and connects it to the platforms you already use.

Set them up in the order below. Each part prepares the ones after it: a program inherits your brand, sends from your domains, and pays its reward through a supplier you choose for it.

In My Extole, [Extole Chat](/product/extole-chat/index) can do much of this with you. Ask it "Set up this account for Acme", with your company's name or website, to bring in your brand. Ask "How's my setup?" to see which parts are in place.

## Brand

Your company name, website, colors, fonts, logo, and icon are account settings. Every program and campaign inherits them, so a new program starts on brand. See [My Account Settings](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/my-account-settings).

## Program Domain

Every account starts on its own Extole program domain, such as `mycompany.extole.io`. A branded domain, such as `refer.mycompany.com`, shows your company in the promotion links and share links your program uses.

Your DNS team adds one CNAME record, and Extole issues and renews the certificate. Your program keeps working on its Extole program domain until the branded domain is in place. See [Extole DNS Requirements](/technical/operational-tasks/account-configuration/extole-dns-requirements).

## Email Sending Domain

Until you set one up, program emails send from Extole's shared address at `email.referral-mail.com`, under your company name. A sending domain of your own lets program emails come from an address at your company's domain.

Your DNS team adds three CNAME records that sign your mail, and includes `spf.extole.io` in your SPF record. Once the domain passes validation, your account sends program emails from it. See [Extole DNS Requirements](/technical/operational-tasks/account-configuration/extole-dns-requirements) for the records and the checks, and [My Account Settings](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/my-account-settings) for the address your emails come from.

## Integrations

Extole integrates with ecommerce, CRM, marketing automation, and other platforms. An integration connects your program to the platforms your site already runs on, so their events and customers reach Extole. The **Partners** section of Technical Docs lists the platforms Extole integrates with.

## Your First Program

A program is your referral use case: who takes part, and what earns a reward. You start one from a template, and it inherits everything above. See [Creating a Program](/guides/platform-overview/creating-a-program).

## Rewards

A program pays its rewards through a reward supplier, such as gift cards, coupons, or account credits. Choose the reward once the program exists. See [Rewards Overview and FAQ](/guides/rewards-management/setting-up-your-rewards/rewards-overview-and-faq).
