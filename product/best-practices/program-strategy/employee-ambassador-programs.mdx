## How Employees Can Take Part

Your employees, agents, and partners talk to customers every day. An employee ambassador program gives them a personal link or QR code and rewards them when the people they bring in sign up or buy. This guide is the in-depth version of [Referral Programs for Employees](/product/product-overview/programs/refer-a-friend/employee-referral-program), which gives the high-level overview. It goes further into how people take part, how they share and track their results, the program models you can build, and how to keep everyone engaged.

Employees can take part in three ways:

- **General advocate.** Employees refer their own friends and family. This runs as its own program, separate from your customer program, with rewards only for employees.
- **In the field.** Employees refer people directly where they work, such as a technician at the end of an install or an associate on the sales floor, by sharing a personal QR code or link on the spot. A telecom provider, for example, lets employees earn rewards with no cap for referring home internet installations, so a technician has a reason to keep asking all year.
- **Three-party program.** Employees ask their customers to share with their friends. The customer (the advocate) and the friend are rewarded as in any referral program, and you choose whether to reward the employee for their part. In a standard example, a flooring design consultant sells a project and gives the customer a personal link to share. The consultant can be credited too, such as with a lead assigned or a deal added to their quota. Read more in [Agent Programs](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/other-acquisition-and-engagement-programs/agent-programs).

Each can run on the [Go Extole app](/product/product-overview/programs/refer-a-friend/go-extole) or on a web dashboard, and the same person can take part in more than one.

## Who Can Be an Ambassador

You decide who takes part. Eligibility runs on [Audiences](/product/product-overview/programs/refer-a-friend/go-extole#target-your-employees), so you can add and remove people one at a time, in bulk, or by rule, using My Extole, the People API, a file upload, or an automated SFTP workflow. That means new hires can be added and departures removed on a schedule. In Go Extole, employees sign in with their company name and email address and confirm with a verification link, so only people on your list get in.

The model fits any team that speaks with customers at the moment they're most satisfied or ready to decide:

- **In-home service teams.** Technicians and installers can turn job-site satisfaction into a referral. Think flooring, roofing, insulation, and home protection.
- **Telecom and fiber teams.** Installers, support technicians, and door-to-door reps can invite a new sign-up the moment the line is live or the problem is solved.
- **Store and branch staff.** Associates, tellers, and branch teams talk to customers face to face, often when they're about to buy or open an account.
- **Sales reps.** Reps can refer businesses they meet, with each referral creating a lead in your CRM and rewards that scale with the size of the deal.
- **Hospitality teams.** Staff at venues and attractions can encourage follow-on visits and referrals.
- **Educators and instructors.** Teachers and tutors can help grow parent-to-parent referral programs.
- **Third-party agents.** Property managers, leasing agents, resellers, and partners aren't employees, but they ask on your behalf in the same way. An account manager who oversees them can have a dashboard too.

The Extole [Employee Ambassadors](https://www.extole.com/employee-ambassadors/) page lists several of these teams.

## Go Extole: The Program in Your Employees' Pocket

[Go Extole](https://www.extole.com/platform/go-extole-app/) delivers an Extole program to your employees' phones, on iOS and Android. It replaces printed cards and separate reports with one app that shares, tracks, and rewards. Its [documentation](/product/product-overview/programs/refer-a-friend/go-extole) covers setup in detail.

For an employee, the experience is short:

1. Download the app and enter their company name and email address.
2. Confirm with the verification link sent by email.
3. Open their personal QR code or share link.
4. Share it with a customer or friend in person, or by text, email, or social.
5. Watch referrals and rewards update in real time.

What the app gives you:

- **Personal QR codes and share links** for each employee, so every sign-up is attributed to the person who shared.
- **A stats dashboard** showing who was referred, which rewardable steps they took, and the status of each reward.
- **In-app reward redemption** through [Reward Bank](/technical/platform-integrations/extensions/reward-bank), or automatic delivery of rewards through Extole.
- **Your branding.** The experience after sign-in can be configured to match your brand, and a fully branded version is available.

The customer's side is the same simple journey every time: they scan the code or tap the link, land on a page built for your offer, complete the action you're rewarding, such as a purchase, subscription, or account opening, and the rewards go out automatically.

Go Extole runs on a new or existing Refer a Friend program, so many programs your employees already use can become app-enabled. You choose the eligible audience in the program's app page settings. The [technical guide](/technical/solutions/go-extole-technical-docs) has the steps, and your CSM can turn it on.

## No App? Use a Web Dashboard

Not every team works from a mobile app. Give employees and agents a web dashboard instead, often linked from their corporate site or intranet. It runs on the same program, audience, and rewards as the app.

How it works:

- **Invite and verify.** A branded welcome email, sent with the [Email Audience](/guides/audiences-and-segmentation/my-audiences/how-to-send-a-promotional-email-to-an-audience) feature, links to the program's share experience. There, employees verify who they are if you require it and receive a personal link, code, or QR code. The [employee program overview](/product/product-overview/programs/refer-a-friend/employee-referral-program) walks through the journey.
- **Use your own login.** If employees already sign in to a corporate portal, your system can pass Extole a signed token (a JSON Web Token, or JWT) when they do. That is Extole's single sign-on method for participants: they go straight to their stats without a separate verification email. Without it, employees confirm their identity with an email link. See [Verifying Consumers](/technical/operational-tasks/security-and-compliance/verifying-consumers#using-json-web-tokens-jwts) for the setup.
- **See their results.** An [Advocate Stats Dashboard](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/add-an-advocate-stats-dashboard) shows each person their referrals, earned rewards, and earned value, plus recent activity. You can show it on the program's sharing microsite, embed it behind login on your own website, such as an employee portal, or build your own with the Extole APIs.
- **Manage the program.** Program dashboards and reports show you participation, acquisition, and your top performers. In partner programs, account managers can have dashboards of their own.

Use the app for mobile teams who share in person, and a web dashboard for desk-based staff, agents, and partners who already work from a portal. Many programs use both.

## More Program Models You Can Build

Beyond those three, the same tools support programs for other groups. They all run on the same Extole program, audience, and rewards tools, so you can start with one and add the others.

- **Sales rep and B2B lead referral.** Reps refer businesses, and each referral creates a lead in your CRM. An HR and payroll software company runs this model with a personal QR code for every employee and gift card rewards that grow with the size of the business that signs.
- **Property manager and partner programs.** Property managers, leasing agents, and other partners promote your service to the people they serve. A major telecom provider runs a dedicated program like this for property managers, movers, tenants, and residents in eligible service areas, alongside its separate employee program. Each audience gets its own program because their needs differ. The [Top Extole Programs for Telecom](/product/best-practices/program-strategy/top-programs-for-telecom) guide goes deeper.
- **On-the-go ambassadors.** Employees or partners with an audience of their own can run an [Ambassador program](/product/product-overview/programs/influencer) through the app to encourage sign-ups.

## Keeping Employees Active

Employee programs rarely fail because the setup is wrong. They fade because people stop thinking about them. Five things keep ambassadors participating.

- **Rewards that match the work.** You can provide coupons, set up gift cards through a partner provider, use direct deposit, or call back to your existing employee reward system by API, file, or webhook. Rewards can be tiered by product or subscription, by number of referrals, or by deal size, and some programs set no cap at all so a top performer keeps earning.
- **Visible progress.** The Go Extole app or a web dashboard shows each employee their referrals and how much they've earned, so they never have to ask for a report. Managers can see results too. In partner programs, a property manager can have a personal dashboard, and the account manager who oversees property managers can have one of their own to compare performance, highlight top performers, and push referrals.
- **Reasons to stay in the game.** Add a monthly drawing based on referral activity, so every referral is another entry. A [sweepstakes](https://www.extole.com/sweepstakes/) is one way to run it. Team bonus tiers and leaderboards add some friendly competition.
- **Training and excitement.** Participation depends mostly on how well the program is promoted inside your company. Send a branded welcome email with the Email Audience feature, add personal QR codes to email signatures and internal dashboards, and train people on when and how to ask. A short walkthrough of the moments to ask, such as right after an install or a closed sale, helps. Employees who are excited about the program ask more often.
- **Constant communication.** Don't stop at launch. Send a monthly email with results, top performers, and any new offer, using the Email Audience feature. Keep the program on your corporate site or intranet where employees already look, and bring it up in team meetings and internal newsletters. People take part when they're regularly reminded the program exists.

Protect the program with the same quality rules you use elsewhere, including employee verification and self-referral prevention, so rewards go only to legitimate referrals.

Track two numbers. Participation rate is the share of eligible employees who take part, and it mostly reflects how well you promote the program internally. Acquisition rate is conversions divided by advocates, and it shows how effective the program is. Both are in your dashboards, along with a view of your top performers.

## Works Across Industries

Employee ambassador programs work in any business with people who talk to customers.

- **Home services.** A technician finishing a flooring, roofing, or insulation job can share a referral code before leaving the driveway.
- **Banks and credit unions.** Branch staff talk to members when they're deciding to open an account. Extole offers in-branch and printable referral options for credit unions and banks (see the [2025 updates](/news/newsletters/2025)).
- **Retail.** Store associates can share a code with shoppers in the aisle or at checkout, and reward the people who bring in new customers.
- **Hospitality and attractions.** Venue staff can encourage guests to come back and bring friends.
- **Education.** Tutors, instructors, and learning centers can expand parent-to-parent referrals.
- **B2B and software.** Sales reps and customer success teams can refer businesses, with leads going straight into your CRM.

A few ideas worth testing:

- **Make a launch a team moment.** When you open a new branch or location, give that team its own offer and a short employee burst, so the people nearest the opening are the loudest voices.
- **Reward specific products.** Tier employee rewards by product or plan, so staff lean toward the offers you most want to grow.
- **Include support and service teams.** People who solve problems for customers meet them at another high point. Add them to the audience, not only sales and field staff.

## Getting Started

1. **Pick your first model and your moment.** Choose the program model above that fits your team, and the specific moment they'll ask, such as the end of an install or a closed sale.
2. **Build the audience.** List who is eligible, whether employees, agents, or partners, and plan how the list will stay current, for example with a scheduled SFTP update.
3. **Set up the program and rewards.** Start from a new or existing Refer a Friend program, decide on the reward and whether to tier it, and choose how rewards are delivered.
4. **Choose how people will share and track.** For mobile field teams, ask your CSM to enable Go Extole, then select the eligible audience in the program's app page. For desk-based staff, agents, or partners, set up a web dashboard on your sharing microsite or behind your own login. Employees can use the [Go Extole login guide](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/go-extole-app-login-guide) if they hit an issue signing in.
5. **Launch inside the company.** Send the welcome email, run a short training on when and how to ask, and hold a kickoff so people are excited from day one.
6. **Keep it visible.** Plan a monthly email and a spot on your corporate site, and add a reason to keep going, such as a monthly drawing, a team bonus, or a leaderboard.
7. **Review every month.** Check participation rate and acquisition rate, recognize your top performers, and use what you learn to add the next model.

When you're ready, your CSM can help you choose the model and configure the app.
