## Overview

[//]: # "What do loyalty members see in an Extole program?"

A loyalty program only works if participants can see what they have earned and understand what it
is worth. This page covers what a member encounters, from enrollment through redemption.

[//]: ___

## Enrollment and Consent

Participants join through an experience you brand and place where it makes sense — an account page,
a landing page, or an in-app screen. Enrollment can also come from your side, by adding participants
to an audience you maintain and sync to Extole.

Treat enrollment and marketing consent as separate decisions. Joining a loyalty program is not by
itself consent to receive marketing email or SMS, and where you capture both, record them
separately. Extole can sync opt-in and opt-out state with your other systems; see the
[Extole Opt-In and Opt-Out Sync Guide](/guides/platform-overview/extole-opt-in-opt-out-sync-guide).

For the compliance obligations that apply to the data you collect, see
[GDPR and CCPA](/product/product-overview/security-and-compliance/gdpr-ccpa).

## How Extole Recognizes a Member

A loyalty balance belongs to a person, so the program depends on Extole resolving each participant
to the same profile every time. Pass your own identifier for the participant along with the events
you send, so activity from your site, your app, and your back-office systems lands on one profile.

Because a balance is valuable, the Reward Bank requires the participant to be a verified consumer
before it will show a balance or accept a redemption. Access from an email link is authenticated
with a JSON Web Token appended to the link. See
[Verifying Consumers](/technical/operational-tasks/security-and-compliance/verifying-consumers).

<Warning>
  A participant who reaches the Reward Bank without being verified sees an empty bank rather than
  their balance. Confirm the verification path works before launch, from both a logged-in page and
  an email link.
</Warning>

## The Balance and Redemption Views

Members interact with their points in two places, and you choose which to use:

| View | Behavior |
| :--- | :--- |
| Redemption Center | Embeds into a page you already own, such as an account or rewards page. |
| Redemption Center Microsite | A standalone branded page, suitable for linking to from email. |

Within either, a member can see:

* **My Rewards** — the rewards they have earned and their current balance.
* **Redemption Options** — what the balance can be exchanged for at the current Redemption Rate.
* **Redemption History** — a record of past redemptions.

Members can redeem everything at once or select specific rewards, subject to the value limits you
configured.

You can also surface a balance outside the Redemption Center. Where you need a balance on your own
page or in your app, read it through the Reward Bank endpoints and render it yourself; see
[Points and Balances](/technical/platform-integrations/extensions/reward-bank/points-and-balances).

## Status and Progress

Extole provides a stats dashboard that shows a participant their activity and the rewards they have
earned. It can be displayed on your sharing landing page, embedded behind login on your own site,
or driven entirely by the APIs for a custom implementation. See
[Add an Advocate Stats Dashboard](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/add-an-advocate-stats-dashboard).

For anything beyond what the dashboard shows — distance to a redemption threshold, a tier badge —
build it from data you already hold: the balance from the Reward Bank endpoints, and the tier
attribute from the participant's profile.

<Info>
  **Planned capability.** PLANNED-CAPABILITY — automatic progress messaging, such as a triggered
  "you are 200 points from your next reward" notification sent without you defining the threshold
  event yourself, is not confirmed as an available feature. Confirm with Product before
  publishing.

  What you can build today: a triggered email on an event you send when a participant crosses a
  threshold, using the same reward and email configuration as any other program.
</Info>

## Ongoing Touchpoints

The messages that keep a loyalty program alive are the recurring ones.

**Earned reward emails** confirm that an action produced points and link the member to the
Redemption Center. See the
[Earned Reward Emails Asset Guide](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/asset-guides/earned-reward-emails-asset-guide).

**Pending reward emails** tell a member that an action has been recognized but the reward is not
released yet, which matters where you hold rewards through a return window. See the
[Pending Reward Email Asset Guide](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/asset-guides/pending-reward-email-asset-guide).

**Redemption emails** confirm a redemption and deliver the reward, unless the reward provider
handles that message.
