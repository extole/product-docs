## Overview

[//]: # "What can loyalty points be redeemed for?"

Points are only worth what a participant can turn them into. Redemption Options are the set of
rewards you make available in the Redemption Center, and the Redemption Rate controls how many
points each one costs.

[//]: ___

## The Redemption Rate

The Redemption Rate is the ratio of accumulated reward value to dollars. By default it is 1:1, so
one point equals one dollar. With a ten-point rate, a participant earns one dollar for every ten
points.

You also set value limits — a minimum and maximum monetary amount per redemption. Align the maximum
with the limit your reward provider enforces. Setting a limit to `0` disables it.

For how to choose a rate, see
[Choosing Your Point-to-Reward Ratio](/guides/strategy-and-best-practices/campaign-optimization/choosing-your-point-to-reward-ratio).

## Reward Options

**Coupons and promotional codes**: Single-use codes uploaded in batches, or static codes. Best
where you want redemption to drive another purchase. See
[How to Reward with Coupons](/guides/rewards-management/setting-up-your-rewards/how-to-reward-with-coupons).

**Account credits and store credit**: Value applied to the participant's account in your own
system. Extole tracks the credit and your systems apply it. See
[How to Reward with Account Credits](/guides/rewards-management/setting-up-your-rewards/how-to-reward-with-account-credits).

**Gift cards**: A wide range of gift card and cash-like rewards through Extole's reward providers,
including open-loop and closed-loop cards. See
[How to Reward with Tango Gift Cards](/guides/rewards-management/setting-up-your-rewards/how-to-reward-with-tango-gift-cards)
and [Sending Rewards with Tremendous](/guides/rewards-management/sending-rewards-with-tremendous).

**Charitable donations**: Participants direct their points to a cause instead of taking value
themselves.

**Physical products**: Fulfilled by you rather than by Extole. Configure a custom reward, and
Extole records the redemption and hands it to your fulfillment process.

**Exclusive access and experiences**: Early access to a product release, an invitation, or a
members-only offer. There is no packaged reward type for this. Build it either as a custom reward
that your systems act on, or by placing redeemers into an audience that gates the experience on
your side.

<Note>
  A reward that you fulfill yourself — a physical product, an experience, an invitation — is a
  custom reward. Extole issues and records it; the delivery happens in your systems.
</Note>

## Program Models

The same components support several program shapes. Choose the one that matches how much control
you want participants to have.

### Choose Your Reward

Participants accumulate points and pick from a catalog of Redemption Options when they are ready.
This is the model the Reward Bank is built around, and it gives participants the most control.
Deferred redemption also spreads reward cost over time rather than paying it out at the moment of
earning.

### Reward on Action

Points are not banked. A qualifying action issues a reward directly — a purchase over a threshold
returns a coupon, a completed survey returns a gift card. There is no redemption step and no
balance for the participant to track. Use this where the reward should feel immediate, or where a
balance would add friction without adding value.

You can also vary earning by tier, without a built-in tier engine. Hold the tier as an attribute
on the participant's profile, or as an audience, and write reward rules that issue more points to
higher tiers — your systems decide when a participant changes tier. Two patterns cover most
programs: a fixed tier set from a client-sent attribute, such as subscriber versus non-subscriber,
and tiers a participant moves between as their lifetime points cross a threshold, tracked as
audience membership. See
[Tiers and Member Status](/technical/platform-integrations/extensions/reward-bank/tiers-and-status).
