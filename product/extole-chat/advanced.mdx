## Overview

Extole Chat works as you. It can only see and change what your My Extole access already allows, and the conversation's access mode sets a second limit on top of that. This page covers those limits, what happens to what you type, and how Extole Chat relates to the other assistants Extole offers.

The [AI Terms](https://my.extole.com/chat/terms) are the agreement for Extole Chat. They supplement the Extole Terms of Service.

## Access Modes

A new conversation starts in **Build**. The mode control sits on the message box. **Full access** is marked so a conversation that can publish is visible before you send.

| Mode | What it can do |
|---|---|
| **Ask** | Look things up, and create safe objects such as reports. |
| **Build** | Everything in **Ask**, plus creating and changing drafts. |
| **Full access** | Everything in **Build**, plus publishing, issuing rewards, and account-level changes. |

You choose the mode. When a request needs a higher one, Extole Chat refuses that step before it runs and asks you to switch. Switching does not redo the refused step. Send the request again after the mode changes.

<Warning>
**Full access** can publish a campaign and issue a reward when you have directed it to. Those actions are recorded as yours. Review them the way you would review a change you made by hand.
</Warning>

Your role still applies. A mode does not grant access you do not already have in My Extole. If you cannot publish a campaign yourself, **Full access** does not publish it for you.

## Review the Answer

Extole Chat can be wrong, incomplete, or out of date. A reply is there to help you work. It is not a report of record, and it is not legal, financial, tax, or compliance advice.

Before you rely on a figure, publish it, or send it outside your organization, confirm it against the report or the screen it came from. A report card in the reply opens that report.

Your organization's policies, and the laws that apply to your programs, still apply to anything the assistant helps you produce.

## Your Data

What you type, the files you attach, and the replies you receive are your content. Extole uses them to provide Extole Chat, to keep the service secure, and to review quality, including conversations you rate.

Your account and program data are not used to train foundation models that are shared with other clients, and they are not shown to other Extole clients. Access stays inside your account, under the same sign-in as the rest of My Extole.

Extole may send a prompt and the account context needed to answer it to a provider that hosts the model. Those providers are bound by data-protection terms consistent with Extole's commitments to you. Extole can change providers as the service changes.

Do not treat a chat as the place a decision is stored. Retention follows the [Extole Privacy Policy](https://www.extole.com/privacy-policy/).

<Warning>
Do not paste an access token, a password, or any other credential into Extole Chat. Do not send consumer personal information beyond what your program already needs, or another client's data.
</Warning>

## Attachments

One message can include up to five files. You can add them from the message box, paste them, or drop them on the message box, and you can send a message that is only files.

PNG, JPEG, GIF, and WebP images wider or taller than 1568 pixels on the long side are resized before they upload. Any file that is not one of those image types, and any image that is still over the limit after resizing, has to be 4 MiB or smaller. Extole Chat names the file when it rejects one.

## Developer Mode

**Developer mode** is a switch in **Conversation actions**, and it stays on for you across sessions. With it on, the thread shows the lookups behind a reply. With it off, you see your messages, the assistant's replies, and errors.

Use it when a reply is surprising and you want to see what was looked up. It does not change what Extole Chat is allowed to do.

## Conversations

History is stored with your sign-in, so the same chats are there when you change browser or device. The list is yours, not the account's: switching accounts shows the same chats, while each new message runs against the account you are in.

There is no control to delete or rename a chat, and the chat list cannot be searched yet. After you have sent 15 messages in one chat, Extole Chat suggests **New chat**. You can dismiss that suggestion for that chat and return to the thread later. A new chat keeps the next task from inheriting a long thread.

While a reply is still running, you can write the next message. It waits, marked **Queued**, and sends when the current turn finishes.
