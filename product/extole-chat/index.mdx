## Overview

Extole Chat is the assistant inside My Extole. You ask in plain language about your programs, your participants, and your results. It looks up your account, explains what it finds, and, when you ask it to, drafts or applies changes.

Extole Chat is for the people who run your programs. It is not a chat your customers see, and it is not a person on your Extole team. A reply can be incomplete or out of date. Confirm a number against your reports before you publish it or send it outside your organization.

If Extole Chat is not in your account yet, contact your Extole team.

## What to Use It For

Use Extole Chat when the question is about your own account and the next step is something you would otherwise do yourself in My Extole.

**Performance.** Ask how a program did over a period you name: shares, conversions, reward spend, or which placements drove results. Extole Chat can run a report and show the result in the conversation, including a chart when a trend or a comparison is easier to see than a table.

**A participant or a reward.** Ask why someone did or did not receive a reward, what a person has earned, or how a referral journey progressed. Name the person. An email address is enough when you have one.

**How something is set up.** Ask how a live campaign works, which quality rules apply, or how a reward is configured. Extole Chat reads your configuration and Extole's documentation.

**A change.** Ask it to draft a program, raise a friend reward, or add a rule. A new conversation starts in **Build**, which can create and edit drafts. Publishing a campaign, issuing a reward, and account-level changes wait until you switch that conversation to **Full access**. See [Access Modes and Data](/product/extole-chat/advanced).

**A working summary.** Ask for a short account of how a program is performing and what is worth a closer look. Treat that summary as a starting point. The reports it cites are the record.

## Features

**One conversation, two places.** Extole Chat opens from the left navigation as a panel beside the page you are on, and the same conversation is available on its own page. Starting in the panel and continuing on the full page keeps the thread. A link in a reply that points at another page in My Extole leaves the panel open on that page.

**Chats you can return to.** Each thread keeps its history. **Recent chats** lists the latest ones, and **All chats** lists the rest. Your chat list follows you. If you work in more than one Extole account, you see the same list in each, and each reply uses the account you are signed in to when you send it.

**Rich replies.** A reply can include lists, links, a chart, a diagram, a profile card, or a report card. A report card shows the report name and whether it is still running, and opens the report.

**The page you are on.** Extole Chat can show the program, person, or report on screen. Include that record when the answer depends on it. When you are not sure it is included, name it in the question.

**Files.** You can attach up to five files to a message, including a screenshot of a site or a creative. Images in PNG, JPEG, GIF, and WebP are resized when a side is longer than 1568 pixels. Any other file has to be 4 MiB or smaller. You can send attachments with no text.

**A mode for each kind of work.** The message box shows **Ask**, **Build**, or **Full access**. **Ask** looks things up and can create safe objects such as reports. **Build** adds creating and changing drafts. **Full access** adds publishing, issuing rewards, and account-level changes. Extole Chat will not raise the mode on its own.

**Feedback.** Each reply has a thumbs-up control, a thumbs-down control, and **File a support ticket**. A thumbs-down can include a short reason. Extole uses those ratings to review quality.
