# Overview

To embed a stats experience on one of your pages, follow the steps below.

Follow these instructions if you want to use Extole's authentication flow. With the authentication flow, an advocate clicks to view their stats, receives an email with a verification link, clicks to verify their email, and then sees their stats. You can also verify who a user is by placing the embedded stats zone behind a login or by using JWTs — see [Verifying Consumers](/technical/operational-tasks/security-and-compliance/verifying-consumers).

## Instructions

The rest of the stats experience is controlled in My Extole.
