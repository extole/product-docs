# Overview

The [Pausing, Ending, and Scheduling Campaigns](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/managing-campaigns/pausing-ending-and-scheduling-campaigns)
guide describes each campaign state from a participant's point of view. This page describes the
same transitions as the REST API performs them, because several of them change more than the
`state` field, and nothing in the response says so. Read `state`, `start_date`, `pause_date`, and
`is_published` back after every call on this page.

## Pause, End, and Stop Take an Optional Date

`POST /v2/campaigns/{campaign_id}/pause`, `/end`, and `/stop` each accept an optional request body
of the form `{"date": "<ISO 8601>"}`.

- With no body, the transition happens now.
- With a `date`, the transition is scheduled for that time and the campaign keeps its current
  state until then.
- A `date` earlier than the campaign's start date is refused with `date_before_start_date`.

Send no body when you mean now. A body that carries no `date` is not an instruction, so read
`state` back before you report that the campaign ended.

## Scheduling a Start Clears a Pause

`POST /v2/campaigns/{campaign_id}/schedule` with a `start_date` sets the start date and clears the
campaign's pause date in the same write. A campaign with a start date and no pause date is in the
Not Launched state, and the platform runs a Not Launched campaign as live: its live controllers
serve traffic, and its journeys continue.

So a scheduled start on a paused campaign is not a resume at that date. It is an immediate go-live.
To hand over from one campaign to another at a fixed time, schedule the old campaign's end with
`POST /end {"date": ...}` and set the new campaign live at that time, rather than scheduling a start
on a paused campaign.

A scheduled start also needs a published campaign. A campaign that was never published stays in
Not Launched when its start date arrives; see the warning on the
[guide](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/managing-campaigns/pausing-ending-and-scheduling-campaigns).

## Pause Keeps the Draft, Archive Empties the Campaign

A pause is applied to both the draft and the published version, so an unpublished draft survives a
pause and is still waiting when the campaign goes live again. Read `is_published` before you pause
a campaign that somebody is editing.

Archiving empties the campaign. Components, flow steps, and transition, reward, and quality rules
are removed, while name, description, tags, theme, and labels are kept. Every later read of the
campaign, direct or with `include_archived=true` on the list, returns this emptied form. The
content as it stood before archiving is reachable only through the version immediately before the
archive, listed by `GET /v2/campaigns/{campaign_id}/versions`. `POST /v2/campaigns/{campaign_id}/unarchive`
restores the campaign with its content, and clears its `start_date`, so a blank start date on a
restored campaign means unknown rather than never launched.

## A Burst Launch Writes the Sibling's Pause Date

Launching a limited-time burst campaign in a program writes a pause date on each live campaign in
the same program, equal to the burst's start date. The campaign record carries no flag that marks
a burst, so a live campaign whose `pause_date` equals a sibling's `start_date` in the same program
is how a burst shows in the API. A later burst overwrites the pause date, so the match finds the
most recent burst only.

## Rewards and Emails Are Gated on Different States

Every controller carries its own list of campaign states it runs in. A reward action enabled on
`LIVE`, `PAUSED`, and `ENDED` pays a reward on an ended campaign, while the earned-reward email
controller beside it, enabled on `LIVE` only, sends nothing. The person's journey then shows a paid
reward followed by a reward-email step with no campaign and no quality. Journeys stay on the
campaign they joined, so this shape appears after every switch from one campaign to another. Read
each controller's enabled states from `GET /v2/campaigns/{campaign_id}/controllers/built` before
you promise that an ended campaign's participants will receive an email.
