# Overview

Most programs start as a copy of a template that already carries a complete flow, as
[Creating a Program](/guides/platform-overview/creating-a-program) describes. When no template
matches the flow you need, start from the **Custom Campaign Template** and assemble the campaign
from components. This page gives the order of the calls.
[Design a Campaign from Components](/technical/integration-overview/design-a-campaign-from-components)
covers what to build: the roles, journeys, business events, and rewards for your program, and
the rules that let Extole do the counting.
[Configuration Concepts](/api-reference/getting-started/configuration-concepts) explains
components, types, and sockets, and draws the tree you are building.

## Start from the Custom Campaign Template

[List duplicatable campaigns](/api-reference/campaigns/list-duplicatable-campaigns) returns the
templates your account can copy. Select the row whose `name` is `Custom Campaign Template`. Its
`program_type` is `empty-campaign`, and other templates can share that `program_type`.

[Duplicate a campaign](/api-reference/campaigns/duplicate-a-campaign) creates your copy as a
draft. Send the `name` of the new campaign and the `program_label` of the program it belongs to.

The copy holds the smallest tree a Flow Builder campaign needs:

| Component | Absolute name | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Root | `/` | The campaign. Its type is `campaign-v10.0`, and it carries the `roles` socket and the campaign-level settings. |
| Placeholder Role | `/placeholder_role` | One role, to rename or replace. |
| Placeholder Journey | `/placeholder_role/placeholder_journey` | One journey in that role. |
| Template Business Event | `/placeholder_role/placeholder_journey/template_business_event` | One business event in that journey. |

It holds no reward.

[Create a campaign](/api-reference/campaigns/create-a-campaign) makes a campaign whose root
component has no type and no sockets. A Flow Builder campaign needs the `campaign-v10.0` type on
its root, and the template supplies it.

## Add Components in Tree Order

You add to a campaign by copying a component into a socket. A child needs its parent, so add
components in this order:

| Order | Component | Parent | Socket on the parent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Role | The root, `/` | `roles` |
| 2 | Journey | A role | `journeys` |
| 3 | Business event | A journey | `businessEvents` |
| 4 | Reward | A role | `rewards` |
| 5 | Reward rule | A reward | `rewardRules` |
| 6 | Action | A business event | `actions` |

For each component:

1. Call [List duplicatable components](/api-reference/components/list-duplicatable-components)
   with `target_component_id` set to the id of the parent and `target_setting_name` set to the
   socket. The response lists only the sources that fit that socket.
2. Choose the source. The template sources are `template_role`, `template_journey`,
   `template_tracked_business_event`, and `reward_core`. When two rows have the same name, take
   the row whose `owner` is `EXTOLE`, which is the source that Extole maintains.
3. Call [Duplicate a component](/api-reference/components/duplicate-a-component) on the source.
   Send `target_campaign_id`, `target_component_absolute_name` for the parent,
   `target_setting_name` for the socket, and `component_display_name` for the copy. The copy
   takes its name from the display name: `Member Joined` becomes `member_joined`.
4. Call [List component settings](/api-reference/components/list-component-settings) on the
   copy, and set the values your program needs.
   [Component Settings Over REST](/technical/platform-integrations/rest-apis/component-settings-over-rest)
   covers how to change a value the copy inherited.

The copy keeps the settings of its source until you change them. It also brings the children
of its source: `reward_core` arrives with two reward rules, and **Publish Event Data** arrives
with a person identification.

For the promotion and invitation steps of a flow, copy these sources:

| Step | Source | Parent | Socket | What arrives with it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The promotion is viewed and clicked | `promotion_viewed` and `promotion_clicked` | A journey of your own | `businessEvents` | The two business events |
| The invitation is shared | The `share` journey | The first person's role | `journeys` | `shared`, the share emails, the share link, and a targeting scenario |
| The invitation is clicked | The `new_customer` journey | The second person's role | `journeys` | `share_clicked` and its landing destination |

Three `sequence` settings put the campaign in the order of your flow in Flow Builder:

- `sequence` on each journey orders the rows, across all the roles. Give each journey a number
  of its own. A copy keeps the number of its source, so set it on every journey that you copy.
- `sequence` on each business event orders the events of a journey.
- `sequence` on each role orders the columns.

## Set the Trigger of Each Business Event

The **Triggered By** setting, `triggerEvent`, names the event that starts the business event.
[How to Set Up Triggered By](/guides/flow-campaigns/how-to-set-up-triggered-by) covers how to
declare the trigger events your systems send, and
[Select components for a component reference list](/api-reference/components/select-components-for-a-component-reference-list)
sets the reference.

While **Triggered By** is empty, the **Triggered by Input Event** rule in the business event's
`triggerRules` socket starts the business event. Copy `input_event` into `triggerRules`, and set
its `triggerEventNames` to the names of the events your systems send.

One event must match every trigger of a business event. So a business event has one starting
trigger: **Triggered By**, or the **Triggered by Input Event** rule. A business event that
another business event or a **Publish Event Data** action starts has **Triggered By** only.

## Add the Rules from Your Design

[Design a Campaign from Components](/technical/integration-overview/design-a-campaign-from-components)
names the rule for each condition in your program. Each rule is a component, and you copy it into
its socket in the same way:

| Rule or action | Parent | Socket on the parent |
|---|---|---|
| A trigger rule, such as **Has Taken Action** with a **Minimum Action Age** | A business event | `triggerRules` |
| A unique event key: the `event_id` data item, with the copy named after the field that holds the key, such as `membership_id` | A business event | `data` |
| A reward rule, such as **Has Taken Action** with a count, or **Reward Limit** | A reward | `rewardRules` |
| **Publish Event Data** | A business event | `actions` |
| A person identification | **Publish Event Data** | `personIdentification` |

**Has Taken Action** exists as a trigger rule and as a reward rule. List the duplicatable
components for the socket you are filling, and the response holds the one that fits it.

## Give Each Reward a Supplier, a Delivery, and an Email

A reward issues when it has a reward supplier, and it reaches the person when it has a delivery
with an email:

1. Call [Create a reward supplier](/api-reference/reward-suppliers/create-a-reward-supplier). For
   a reward that your own systems deliver, such as an account credit or a free month, send
   `reward_supplier_type` `CUSTOM_REWARD`, a `type` of `ACCOUNT_CREDIT` or `LOYALTY_POINTS`,
   a `face_value_type`, and a `face_value`.
   [How to Reward with Account Credits](/guides/rewards-management/setting-up-your-rewards/how-to-reward-with-account-credits)
   describes custom rewards.
2. Call [Update a component setting](/api-reference/components/update-a-component-setting) to
   set the reward's `rewardSupplierId` to the `id` of the supplier.
3. Select the business event that earns the reward in the reward's `triggerEvent`.
4. Copy `reward_issuer`, **Reward Emails**, into the reward's `rewardIssuer` socket. Then copy
   `reward_email` into the `sendActions` socket of that copy.

### Use a Placeholder Supplier Until Fulfillment Is Connected

A custom reward with `auto_fulfillment_enabled` and `reward_email_auto_send_enabled` set to
`true` is fulfilled and sent inside Extole, with no call to your systems. Create one for each
reward in a new campaign. A test person then earns the reward and receives the reward email
before your fulfillment exists. Replace the placeholder when your fulfillment is ready.

## Remove the Components You Do Not Use

[Delete a component](/api-reference/components/delete-a-component) removes a component and its
children. Delete the placeholder role once your own role replaces it, and its journey and
business event go with it. Deleting the root component archives the campaign.

## Read the Campaign Back and Test It

[Get a built campaign](/api-reference/campaigns/get-a-built-campaign) returns the campaign with
every setting evaluated. Read it to confirm that each business event has its trigger and each
reward has its rules.

The campaign stays a draft until you publish it.
[Test a Campaign Draft Over REST](/technical/platform-integrations/rest-apis/test-a-campaign-draft-over-rest)
covers how to send test events to the draft. In the `test` container, a reward comes from your
account's test reward supplier. To issue the reward from the supplier that the reward names,
such as a placeholder, send the event to the campaign sandbox with the `production` container:
`campaign-<campaign id>-production`.
[Campaign States Over REST](/technical/platform-integrations/rest-apis/campaign-states-over-rest)
covers how to publish and launch the campaign.
