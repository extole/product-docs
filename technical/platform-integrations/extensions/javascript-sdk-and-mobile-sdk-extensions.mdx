# Overview

When your website or app asks Extole for something to show, such as a banner, an overlay, or an embedded share experience, it requests a **zone** by name. A Flow Builder call-to-action or business event that listens for that zone selects it in [Triggered By](/guides/flow-campaigns/how-to-set-up-triggered-by), and it can only select a zone that an extension declares:

- The **Javascript SDK** extension declares the zones your website requests.
- The **Mobile SDK** extension declares the zones your iOS and Android apps request, separately for each platform.

These extensions describe your integration to your campaigns. They don't change the code on your site or in your app. For the code side, see [JavaScript SDK](/technical/platform-integrations/javascript-sdk/index) and [Mobile SDKs](/technical/platform-integrations/mobile-sdks/index).

Install one copy of each extension. A second copy splits your declarations and makes the **Triggered By** list harder to read. To install one, open [Partners](https://my.extole.com/integrations) in My Extole, select the **Extensions** tab, click **+ New Extension**, and select **Javascript SDK** or **Mobile SDK**.

## Javascript SDK

The **Javascript SDK** extension has two lists of zones, and they do different jobs.

- **Tagged CTAs**: zones your own site code requests, through a marketing tag or a `createZone` call. A Tagged CTA only declares the zone so a campaign can select it. It does not make the SDK request the zone.
- **Tagless CTAs**: zones the SDK requests for you, with no code on your page. Each **Tagless CTA** has a **Zone Name**, a **URL Filter** that limits which pages it appears on (blank means every page), and an optional **Anchor Element ID** that sets where on the page it is added. A Tagless CTA can also be selected in **Triggered By**.

If your page already requests a zone, declare it under **Tagged CTAs**. If you want a zone to appear without changing your page, add a **Tagless CTA**.

### Zones Declared by Default

A new **Javascript SDK** extension declares these zones under **Tagged CTAs**: `confirmation`, `embedded_share_experience`, `global_footer`, `global_header`, `mobile_footer`, `mobile_header`, `mobile_menu`, `overlay`, `stats_embedded`. They cover the Refer a Friend programs on your website, including your mobile website.

Other programs request zones beyond this set, such as `homepage_banner` or `show_reward_banner`. [What Each Program Needs](/guides/flow-campaigns/how-to-set-up-triggered-by#what-each-program-needs) lists them.

### App Type

Every zone the **Javascript SDK** declares carries the app type `javascript_sdk`, which is what the SDK reports on your website. The value is one expert setting for the whole extension, so changing it changes every zone at once. Leave it at `javascript_sdk` unless your Extole team tells you otherwise.

## Mobile SDK

The **Mobile SDK** extension holds its zones under **Platforms**, with one platform for each app:

| Platform | App type it reports |
|---|---|
| **iOS** | `mobile-sdk-ios` |
| **Android** | `mobile_sdk_android` |

Each platform has its own **Tagged CTAs**, and every zone under a platform takes that platform's app type. A zone your iOS app and your Android app both request is declared twice, once under each platform.

The extension also has **Experiences**: the mobile zones the app loads, including bootstrap and the call to action. These aren't selected through **Triggered By**.

### Zones Declared by Default

A new **Mobile SDK** extension declares these zones under **iOS** and again under **Android**: `advocate_mobile_experience`, `mobile_footer`, `mobile_header`, `mobile_menu`.

## The Same Zone From Your Website and Your Apps

`mobile_footer`, `mobile_header`, and `mobile_menu` are declared in both extensions: on the **Javascript SDK** for your mobile website, and on the **Mobile SDK** under each platform for your apps. Each is a distinct declaration with its own app type, and a call-to-action serves only the requests whose app type matches a declaration it has selected.

To show a call-to-action everywhere it is requested, select every declaration of its zone in **Triggered By**. If it shows in one place and not another, see [Why a Zone Shows on Your Website but Not in Your App](/technical/troubleshooting/why-a-zone-shows-on-your-website-but-not-in-your-app).

## Add a Zone

1. Open [Partners](https://my.extole.com/integrations) in My Extole, select the **Extensions** tab, and open **Javascript SDK** or **Mobile SDK**.
2. Add the zone with the exact name your site or app requests:
   - On the **Javascript SDK**, add it under **Tagged CTAs**.
   - On the **Mobile SDK**, open **Platforms**, then **iOS** or **Android**, and add it under that platform's **Tagged CTAs**. Repeat for the other platform if both apps request it.
3. Publish the extension.
4. In each campaign that should show the zone, select it in **Triggered By** and publish the campaign. A campaign created before you declared the zone does not pick it up on its own.

## Extensions Installed Before Triggered By

A **Javascript SDK** or **Mobile SDK** extension installed before Triggered By was available may not declare the default zones above, so none of its zones appear in **Triggered By**. Add the missing zones to that copy, as in [Add a Zone](#add-a-zone), rather than installing a second one. If the extension gives you no way to add them, contact your Extole team.
