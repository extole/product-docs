## Overview

Extole Offers for Stripe rewards people through the Stripe account you already use. A reward is either a credit on the Stripe customer's balance or a single-use promotion code for a coupon you already created in Stripe. Extole decides whether a reward is earned, and Extole calls Stripe to issue it. There is no separate payout system.

Stripe is where Extole issues rewards, not where it learns about purchases. Conversions keep coming from your program's existing Extole integration: the JavaScript tag, server-to-server events, or another integration. Extole doesn't read payments, refunds, or cancellations from Stripe.

The integration has two sides:

- **The Extole Offers app** in Stripe. **Sign in with Extole** on its settings page links the app to your Extole account.
- **The Extole Stripe integration** in My Extole. Its Connect setting signs you in to Stripe and approves Extole Offers, so Extole can call Stripe for your account. Extole keeps that connection current; you don't paste a Stripe key.

## Finish Setup

If you installed Extole Offers from the Stripe App Marketplace, Stripe sent you here. The app is installed, but Extole can't issue rewards until you finish [setting up the integration](#set-up-the-integration): sign in with Extole in the app, connect Stripe in My Extole, and add a reward supplier to your program.

Don't have an Extole account? Contact support@extole.com or [request a demo](https://www.extole.com/demo).

## Prerequisites

| Requirement | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Extole account | A My Extole user who can install and publish integrations. |
| Extole program | A program that already receives conversions from an Extole integration, such as the JavaScript tag or server-to-server events. |
| Stripe account | An active Stripe account. Use test mode or a Stripe sandbox to test. |
| Stripe coupon | Required only for promotion-code rewards. Create the coupon in Stripe before you configure the supplier. |

## Set Up the Integration

1. Install [Extole Offers](https://marketplace.stripe.com/apps/com.extole.stripe-offers-v4) in Stripe from the Stripe App Marketplace, and approve its permissions. Install it where you want rewards issued: your live account, its test mode, or a sandbox.
2. Open the app's settings page in the Stripe Dashboard and click **Sign in with Extole**. Sign in with your My Extole user. The app keeps only your email and Extole client id; it never holds an Extole API token.
3. In My Extole, open **Partners** > **Stripe**, and click **Install** if the integration isn't installed yet. On the **Configuration** tab, click the Connect setting that matches where you installed the app:
   - **Connect a live Stripe account**
   - **Connect a Stripe test mode account**
   - **Connect a Stripe sandbox**

   Stripe asks you to sign in and approve Extole Offers, then returns you to My Extole.
4. Open the integration's **Rewards** tab and add a [Stripe reward supplier](#reward-types). For promotion codes, pick the **Stripe Coupon**. Then open **Programs**, edit the program, and attach the supplier to the reward.

Connect only the one that matches where you installed Extole Offers. If more than one is connected, Extole uses the live account first, then test mode, then the sandbox.

**Sign out** on the app's settings page removes the Extole sign-in from the app. It doesn't disconnect Stripe from Extole.

### Disconnect or Uninstall

To stop rewards, disconnect the Stripe connection on the integration's **Configuration** tab in My Extole. Uninstalling Extole Offers in Stripe also ends Extole's access and stops rewards. Either way, rewards fail until you connect again.

## Identity

A Customer Balance credit goes to a Stripe customer, identified by `partner_user_id`, the Stripe customer id (`cus_…`). Send it on the Extole event that earns the reward, usually the conversion your existing integration already sends. A promotion code doesn't need one. See [Advocates and Friends](#advocates-and-friends).

To find an advocate's Stripe customer without sending it, copy the advocate lookup controller into your marketing campaign and set its **Journey Names** to the campaign's advocate journey. When the advocate creates a share link, it looks up Stripe customers by the advocate's email. One match writes `partner_user_id`. Several matches use the earliest customer Stripe returns. No match leaves the person without a Stripe customer id.

Stripe's customer search can lag about a minute behind a new customer. A lookup for someone who became a Stripe customer moments earlier can find no match.

## Reward Types

Add a supplier from the integration's **Rewards** tab, then attach it to the reward in your program.

### Advocates and Friends

Either Stripe reward can go to advocates or friends:

| Reward | Who can receive it | Needs a Stripe customer id |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stripe Promotion Code** | Anyone | No |
| **Stripe Customer Balance** | Anyone with a Stripe customer | Yes: `partner_user_id` (`cus_…`) on the person |

A balance credit gets the person's Stripe customer id from `partner_user_id` on the Extole event that earns the reward, usually the conversion your existing integration already sends. For advocates, the [advocate lookup](#identity) also finds it by email when they create a share link. Without a Stripe customer id, the credit fails with a reward message saying the person has no Stripe customer id.

A typical setup is a balance credit for the advocate and a promotion code for the friend. A friend credit also works when the conversion event carries the friend's `cus_` id.

### Customer Balance

**Stripe Customer Balance** credits the Stripe customer's balance, so the credit reduces their next invoice. Set **Face Value Type** to the currency and **Face Value** to the amount. Turn on **Use Dynamic Value** when the credit should be a percentage of the rewardable event. Set that percentage to 100 to use this supplier as a [Reward Bank](/technical/platform-integrations/extensions/reward-bank/reward-bank-configuration-guide) redemption reward.

Extole posts a negative balance transaction on the customer, in the currency's minor units:

- Most currencies are scaled by 100.
- Zero-decimal currencies, such as Japanese yen, Korean won, and Chilean peso, are not scaled.
- Three-decimal currencies, such as Kuwaiti dinar, are scaled by 1,000, as Stripe requires.

The Stripe balance transaction id becomes the reward's partner reward id. The person must have a Stripe customer id. Without one, the reward fails with a message saying the person has no Stripe customer id.

### Promotion Code

**Stripe Promotion Code** issues a single-use promotion code for a coupon you already created. The code becomes the reward's partner reward id, so emails, the reward center, and reports show it the way they show any other coupon.

Select the **Stripe Coupon**, and set **Face Value Type** and **Face Value** to match it. They control what Extole shows the person; the coupon in Stripe sets the actual discount. Optionally turn on **First Purchase Only**, or set **Expires After Days**. Zero days means the code doesn't expire.

Extole creates the code with a redemption limit of one and records the Extole reward id in its metadata. When the person has a Stripe customer id, the code is restricted to that customer. Without one, the code works for any customer who has it.

Dynamic value isn't available. A Stripe coupon's amount is fixed when you create it. Use Customer Balance when the amount has to follow the event or a Reward Bank redemption.

### Retries and Idempotency

Every request to Stripe carries an idempotency key built from the Extole reward id. A retried reward replays the first request instead of issuing a second credit or code. Extole retries when Stripe is unavailable or rate-limits the request, and marks the reward failed if the last retry fails too. When Stripe rejects the request, Extole marks the reward failed with Stripe's error message.

## Limits

- **Rewards only.** Extole doesn't read purchases, refunds, or cancellations from Stripe. Conversions come from your program's existing Extole integration.
- **One Stripe account per integration.** Extole issues rewards in the account you connect: live, test mode, or a sandbox.
- **Refunds don't reverse rewards.** Extole doesn't take back a credit or a code on its own.
- **Redemptions aren't tracked.** Extole doesn't mark a promotion code redeemed when a customer uses it. Stripe shows the code's redemptions.
- **Customer Balance needs a Stripe customer.** The person must have a `cus_` id when the reward is fulfilled.
- **Promotion codes use fixed coupons.** The discount is the coupon's amount in Stripe.
- **The coupon list shows 100 coupons.** **Stripe Coupon** lists the first 100 coupons in your Stripe account.
- **The advocate lookup can lag.** Stripe's customer search can take about a minute to find a new customer.

## FAQs

**Does the person need to exist in Stripe before they can receive a reward?** For Customer Balance, yes. Send their `partner_user_id` on the event that earns the reward, or, for an advocate, resolve them with the advocate lookup before the reward is earned. A promotion code can be issued to anyone, with no Stripe customer.

**Can friends get a balance credit?** Yes, when the conversion event carries the friend's `cus_` id as `partner_user_id`. Otherwise give friends a promotion code.

**Do I need a webhook in Stripe?** No. Extole doesn't receive Stripe events. It only calls Stripe to issue rewards.

**What happens if the Stripe connection stops working?** Rewards fail with Stripe's error. Open the integration's **Configuration** tab and connect again.

**Can a promotion code be a percentage of the cart?** Only if the coupon is a percentage coupon in Stripe. For a percentage of the cart as credit, use Customer Balance with dynamic value.
