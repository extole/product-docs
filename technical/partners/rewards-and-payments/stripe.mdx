## Overview

Extole Offers for Stripe rewards people through the Stripe account you already use. A reward is either a credit on the Stripe customer's balance or a single-use promotion code for a coupon you already created in Stripe. Stripe sends your payment and subscription events to Extole, Extole decides whether a reward is earned, and Extole calls Stripe to issue it. There is no separate payout system.

The integration has two sides:

- **The Extole Stripe integration** in My Extole. Its **Connect to Stripe** button signs you in to Stripe and approves Extole Offers, so Extole can call Stripe for your account. Extole keeps that connection current; you don't paste a Stripe key.
- **The Extole Offers app** in Stripe. **Sign in with Extole** links the app to your Extole account, and its settings page walks you through the rest of setup and checks your webhook destination.

You can start from either side.

## Finish Setup

If you installed Extole Offers from the Stripe App Marketplace, Stripe sent you here. The app is installed, but Extole can't issue rewards or receive events until you finish these steps:

1. In My Extole, open **Partners** > **Stripe**. Click **Install** if the integration isn't installed yet.
2. On the integration's **Configuration** tab, click **Connect to Stripe**, sign in to Stripe, and approve Extole Offers.
3. [Create the webhook destination](#create-the-webhook-destination) in Stripe.
4. [Add a reward supplier](#reward-types) and attach it to your campaign.

Then open Extole Offers in the Stripe Dashboard and click **Sign in with Extole** to see the setup checklist.

Don't have an Extole account? Contact support@extole.com or [request a demo](https://www.extole.com/demo).

## Install the Integration

### Start From Extole

1. In My Extole, open **Partners** > **Stripe** and click **Install**.
2. On the **Configuration** tab, click **Connect to Stripe**. Stripe asks you to sign in, choose the account, and approve the permissions Extole Offers requests. Stripe installs the app on that account and returns you to My Extole.
3. [Create the webhook destination](#create-the-webhook-destination).
4. Open the **Rewards** tab and add a supplier. Then open **Programs**, edit the campaign, and attach that supplier to the reward.

### Start From the Stripe App Marketplace

1. Install [Extole Offers](https://marketplace.stripe.com/apps/com.extole.stripe-offers-v4) from the Stripe App Marketplace and approve its permissions.
2. Open the app's settings page in the Stripe Dashboard and click **Sign in with Extole**. Sign in with your My Extole user. The app keeps only your email and Extole client id; it never holds an Extole API token.
3. Click **Connect**. My Extole opens your Stripe integration. Click **Connect to Stripe** there to finish the connection.
4. Follow the rest of the checklist on the settings page: create the webhook destination, then wait for the destination check to turn green.

**Sign out** on the settings page removes the Extole sign-in from the app. It doesn't disconnect Stripe from Extole.

### Disconnect or Uninstall

To disconnect, open the integration's **Configuration** tab in My Extole and disconnect the Stripe connection. Removing Extole Offers in Stripe also ends Extole's access. Either way, rewards fail until you click **Connect to Stripe** again. Delete the Extole destination in Stripe so Stripe stops sending events.

## Create the Webhook Destination

Stripe sends events to Extole through a webhook destination you create in your Stripe account. Extole doesn't create it for you.

1. In **Security Center**, create an access token with the **User Support** scope. Use it for this webhook only.
2. In the Stripe Dashboard, open **Developers** > **Webhooks** and add a destination. Use the endpoint URL below, with your token in place of `YOUR_ACCESS_TOKEN`.
3. Select the seven events in the table below. The app's settings page lists them with a copy button for each.
4. Save the destination. In Extole Offers, the destination check marks each event as found or missing.

```
https://api.extole.io/v6/events/stripe_event?access_token=YOUR_ACCESS_TOKEN
```

| Stripe event | Why Extole needs it |
| --- | --- |
| `checkout.session.completed` | A paid one-time Checkout Session counts as a purchase. |
| `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded` | A delayed Checkout payment, such as a bank debit, succeeded and counts as a purchase. |
| `invoice.paid` | A subscription's first paid invoice counts as a purchase. |
| `payment_intent.succeeded` | A payment made without Checkout or Billing. It counts only when **Payment Intent Purchases** is on. |
| `customer.subscription.deleted` | A subscription was canceled. |
| `charge.refunded` | A charge was fully or partly refunded. |
| `customer.discount.created` | A customer applied a promotion code, so Extole can mark the matching reward redeemed. |

The token is part of the URL, so anyone who sees the URL can send events to your account. **User Support** is the lowest scope the events endpoint accepts. Don't reuse a token that has a higher scope or serves another integration.

To rotate the token, or if the URL leaks, create a new **User Support** access token, update the destination URL in Stripe, and then delete the old token in **Security Center**.

## Events Extole Receives

Extole turns each Stripe event into an Extole input event. Purchases become **Conversion**, so a Flow Builder campaign's Converted event works without editing **Triggered By**. Cancellations and refunds become integration events you can pick as **Triggered By** for your own business events.

| Stripe event | When | Extole input event | `partner_conversion_id` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `checkout.session.completed`, `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded` | The session is a one-time payment (`mode` is `payment`) and is paid | `conversion` | The payment intent id |
| `payment_intent.succeeded` | **Payment Intent Purchases** is on | `conversion` | The payment intent id |
| `invoice.paid` | The invoice belongs to a subscription and `amount_paid` is more than zero | `conversion` | The subscription id |
| `customer.subscription.deleted` | Always | `stripe_customer_subscription_deleted` (**Subscription Canceled**) | — |
| `charge.refunded` | Always | `stripe_charge_refunded` (**Refund**) | The refunded payment intent id |
| `customer.discount.created` | Always | `stripe_customer_discount_created`, read by the integration's redemption controller | — |
| Any other event, or a row above when its condition isn't met | — | `stripe_` plus the Stripe type with dots as underscores | — |

### Purchases

Each payment becomes one Conversion:

- **Checkout and Payment Links.** A paid one-time Checkout Session is the purchase. A Checkout Session for a subscription is counted from its first paid invoice instead.
- **Subscriptions.** `invoice.paid` carries the subscription id as `partner_conversion_id`, and Converted keeps only the first event per id. So a subscription counts once, when it first pays: after a trial, or on the first invoice. Renewals don't count again.
- **Payment Intent Purchases.** Leave this setting off if you take payments through Checkout, Payment Links, or Billing. Those payments also emit `payment_intent.succeeded`, so turning it on counts them twice. Turn it on only when you create payment intents yourself.
- **One-off invoices.** An invoice that isn't part of a subscription isn't a Conversion. To count those payments, turn on **Payment Intent Purchases**, with the caution above.

`cart_value` is the money moved, as a decimal in major units, for example `20.00`. It comes from `amount_total` on a Checkout Session, `amount_received` on a payment intent, `amount_paid` on an invoice, and `amount_refunded` on a refund. It drives revenue reporting, percentage rewards, and minimum cart value rules.

### Subscription Canceled and Refund

**Subscription Canceled** is recorded when a subscription ends, at period end or immediately.

**Refund** is recorded for every `charge.refunded`, full or partial. Its `refund_full` field is `true` when the whole charge was refunded and `false` for a partial refund. To trigger a business event on full refunds only, add an **Event Data Comparison** trigger rule: event parameter `refund_full`, comparison equals, value `true`. Its `partner_conversion_id` is the refunded payment intent, which matches the Conversion of a one-time purchase. A subscription's Conversion carries the subscription id instead.

### Event Data

Extole copies these fields onto every event when Stripe sends them:

- `partner_user_id`: the Stripe customer id, when it starts with `cus_`.
- `email`: from the Checkout customer details, `customer_email`, the billing details, or `receipt_email`, in that order.
- `currency`: as an uppercase ISO code.
- `status`, `subscription`, `stripe_event_id`, `stripe_event_type`, and `stripe_object_id`.
- `promotion_code_id`: on a Checkout Session or discount that used a promotion code.

Every Stripe event also carries the app type `extole-stripe-app`.

## Identity

Extole matches a Stripe event to a person by `partner_user_id`, which is the Stripe customer id (`cus_`), and by `email` when the event has one.

A friend who buys through Stripe Checkout is usually matched by email. To match an advocate before they buy, copy the advocate lookup controller into your marketing campaign. On a share, it looks up Stripe customers by the person's email. One match writes `partner_user_id`. Several matches use the earliest customer Stripe returns. No match leaves the person without a Stripe customer id.

Stripe's customer search can lag about a minute behind a new customer. A lookup for someone who became a Stripe customer moments earlier can find no match.

## Reward Types

Add a supplier from the integration's **Rewards** tab, then attach it to the reward in your campaign.

### Customer Balance

**Stripe Customer Balance** credits the Stripe customer's balance, so the credit reduces their next invoice. Set **Face Value Type** to the currency and **Face Value** to the amount. Turn on **Use Dynamic Value** when the credit should be a percentage of the rewardable event. Set that percentage to 100 to use this supplier as a [Reward Bank](/technical/platform-integrations/extensions/reward-bank/reward-bank-configuration-guide) redemption reward.

Extole posts a negative balance transaction on the customer, in the currency's minor units:

- Most currencies are scaled by 100.
- Zero-decimal currencies, such as Japanese yen, Korean won, and Chilean peso, are not scaled.
- Three-decimal currencies, such as Kuwaiti dinar, are scaled by 1,000, as Stripe requires.

The Stripe balance transaction id becomes the reward's partner reward id. The person must have a Stripe customer id. Without one, the reward fails.

### Promotion Code

**Stripe Promotion Code** issues a single-use promotion code for a coupon you already created. The code becomes the reward's partner reward id, so emails, the reward center, and reports show it the way they show any other coupon.

Select the **Stripe Coupon**, and set **Face Value Type** and **Face Value** to match it. They control what Extole shows the person; the coupon in Stripe sets the actual discount. Optionally turn on **First Purchase Only**, or set **Expires After Days**. Zero days means the code doesn't expire.

Extole creates the code with a redemption limit of one and records the Extole reward id in its metadata. When the person has a Stripe customer id, the code is restricted to that customer. Without one, the code works for any customer who has it.

Dynamic value isn't available. A Stripe coupon's amount is fixed when you create it. Use Customer Balance when the amount has to follow the event or a Reward Bank redemption.

### Retries and Idempotency

Every request to Stripe carries an idempotency key built from the Extole reward id. A retried reward replays the first request instead of issuing a second credit or code. Extole retries when Stripe is unavailable or rate-limits the request, and marks the reward failed if the last retry fails too. When Stripe rejects the request, Extole marks the reward failed with Stripe's error message.

## How a Promotion Code Is Redeemed

1. Extole issues the code and stores it as the reward's partner reward id. The reward is earned.
2. Stripe sends `customer.discount.created`, or a Checkout Session completes with a promotion code. Extole reads `promotion_code_id` and asks Stripe for that code.
3. When the code's metadata holds an Extole reward id, Extole redeems that reward. Emails and reports then show it as redeemed.

If Stripe can't find the code, or the code wasn't issued by Extole, nothing changes and the reward stays earned.

## Limits

- **One Stripe account per integration.** Each connection is for the Stripe account you approve in **Connect to Stripe**, in test or live mode.
- **No automatic events.** Extole doesn't create the webhook destination. Until you create it, no purchases, refunds, or redemptions reach Extole.
- **Two conversion sources count twice.** If another source, such as Shopify or the JavaScript SDK, also sends `conversion` for the same purchase, both count. Add a trigger rule on `stripe_event_type` or the app type `extole-stripe-app` to narrow Converted to one source.
- **First payment only.** A subscription counts once, on its first paid invoice. Renewals don't create a Conversion.
- **Refunds don't reverse rewards.** Refund is an event you can act on in your campaign. Extole doesn't take back a credit or a code on its own.
- **Invoices with no payment aren't purchases.** A zero-total invoice, such as a trial, moves no money and doesn't count.
- **Customer Balance needs a Stripe customer.** The person must have a `cus_` id when the reward is fulfilled.
- **Promotion codes use fixed coupons.** The discount is the coupon's amount in Stripe.
- **The coupon list shows 100 coupons.** **Stripe Coupon** lists the first 100 coupons in your Stripe account.
- **One-off invoices aren't purchases.** Only subscription invoices become a Conversion. Turn on **Payment Intent Purchases** to count payments made outside Checkout and subscriptions.
- **Redelivered Refund and Subscription Canceled events aren't deduplicated.** Stripe can deliver an event more than once, and Extole records each delivery. A business event triggered by Refund should be unique by `partner_conversion_id`, and one triggered by Subscription Canceled by `subscription`.
- **The advocate lookup can lag.** Stripe's customer search can take about a minute to find a new customer.

## FAQs

**Does the person need to exist in Stripe before they can receive a reward?** For Customer Balance, yes. Create the customer in Stripe first, or resolve them with the advocate lookup before the reward is earned. A promotion code can be issued without one.

**What happens if the Stripe connection stops working?** Rewards fail with Stripe's error. Open the integration's **Configuration** tab and click **Connect to Stripe** again.

**Why isn't the destination check green?** The webhook destination is missing, its URL is different, or one of the seven events isn't selected. Compare the destination in Stripe with [Create the Webhook Destination](#create-the-webhook-destination).

**Can a promotion code be a percentage of the cart?** Only if the coupon is a percentage coupon in Stripe. For a percentage of the cart as credit, use Customer Balance with dynamic value.
