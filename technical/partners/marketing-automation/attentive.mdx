## Overview

[//]: # "Does Extole integrate with Attentive?"

Attentive is the most comprehensive text message marketing solution, driving 18.5% of total online revenue for businesses by creating thoughtful SMS experiences. Using real-time behavioral data, Attentive automatically sends engaging text messages to each subscriber at every step of the customer lifecycle. Integrating Extole and Attentive allows you to send and receive events such as subscriptions (i.e., opt-ins) and reward events to enable swift communication with customers via SMS.

## Use Cases

[//]: # "How can I leverage the Extole integration with Attentive?"

When a customer, advocate, or referred friend joins your Extole-powered engagement programs, opted-in phone numbers are automatically sent to Attentive along with email addresses, earned coupon rewards, and personal share links.

Drive new customer acquisition by having your best customers refer others who are just like them. Combine the Attentive platform with Extole insights to send personalized refer-a-friend offers to your top performing advocates or to referred friends who recently made their first purchase.

With Extole insights available in the Attentive platform, such as who’s sharing their favorite products with friends, who’s experiencing your brand for the first time, and who has influence, you’ll have an increased ability to personalize your SMS communications and increase your conversion rates.

## Integration

[//]: # "How do I set up the Extole integration with Attentive?"

### Connect Your Extole and Attentive Accounts

Log in to your My Extole account and navigate to the [Partners](https://my.extole.com/partners) page. From here, select the Attentive tile and click the Connect button to initiate the integration process. 

Extole will guide you through a few simple steps, one of which will prompt you to give Extole permission to connect with your Attentive account. Depending on when you last logged in to Extole, you may need to reverify your identity. This is normal.

### Select Your Campaigns

To get your integration up and running, you’ll need to choose the campaigns for which you would like the Attentive integration enabled. You must select at least one campaign, and the campaigns you select must be up-to-date. Once you’ve completed your integration, work with your Customer Success Manager to properly configure the creatives and variables for each campaign you’ve selected.

![Screenshot showing To get your integration up and running, you’ll need to choose the campaigns for which you would like the Attentive integration enabled. You must select at...](/images/extole/8a5625cc4edceef3d6f7e815fdadf14fe8fb1d7beda88d685bba9cb41995ad18.png "Screen Shot 2022-10-17 at 12.00.50 PM.png")

Campaigns that do not have the updated phone number capture variable necessary for the integration will appear grayed out, with the following message: 

<img src="/images/extole/d4c15e4895ebe0f691f2c55a1a6a0dc86221940b7d58da652b8eb2ee80cae173.png" alt="Screenshot showing Campaigns that do not have the updated phone number capture variable necessary for the integration will appear grayed out, with the following message" />

Getting a campaign updated is a quick process. Just reach out to your Extole Customer Success Manager and they will be happy to help you enable the required variable for your campaigns.

<Note>
  **Check That Both Experiences Capture a Phone Number**

  Extole sends a subscription to Attentive only for a person who already has a phone number on their profile. The advocate share experience and the friend landing experience collect that number separately, and either one can be switched off — so a campaign can be selectable here while one or both of its experiences never ask for a number, and Attentive hears nothing from that part of the program.

  Ask your Customer Success Manager to confirm both experiences capture a phone number and an SMS opt-in before you set a launch date. On some campaigns this is a setting. On an older custom-built creative it is an edit to the creative itself, which is scoped work rather than a quick update.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **Publish each campaign you select**

  Selecting a campaign adds the Attentive integration to that campaign as an unpublished change. The integration does not send or receive anything for that campaign until you open it and publish it. Publishing also ships any other unpublished changes the campaign is holding, so review them first.
</Warning>

### Finish Your Configuration

Once you’ve selected your campaigns for the integration, proceed to the Configurations tab. Here you will need to enter the Extole Sign Up Source ID that you collected from your Attentive account. The custom events webhook and subscriptions webhook are automatically created and do not require any configuration on your end.

<img src="/images/extole/33292feea92c88f9e468fdb15a67daaad2ad71699a0e7846ac285f0fe12463d5.png" alt="Screenshot showing Once you’ve selected your campaigns for the integration, proceed to the Configurations tab. Here you will need to enter the Extole Sign Up Source ID that you..." />

Without a Sign Up Source ID, Extole cannot tell Attentive which sign-up unit a captured phone number belongs to, so subscriptions are not created.

### Confirm the Integration Is Live

Check all three before you treat the integration as connected:

1. Every campaign on the **Campaigns** tab that you selected has been published, with no unpublished changes outstanding.
2. The **Configurations** tab shows your Attentive Sign Up Source ID.
3. A test phone capture in one of those campaigns produces a new subscriber in Attentive. Test the advocate and the friend paths separately — they capture the number in different experiences.

### Send a Sample Event Without Publishing

[//]: # "How do I send a test event to Attentive before publishing the campaign?"

Attentive's journey builder only offers an event's properties as macros once it has seen that event, so teams often want a sample of each event before the referral program goes live.

Real referral activity cannot produce those calls yet. The Attentive steps live inside the campaign, so they run only in that campaign's published version — until you publish, no share, sign-up or earned reward reaches Attentive no matter how the campaign is exercised.

Send the sample straight through the webhook instead, which needs no campaign change:

```bash
curl --request POST "$EXTOLE_API_HOST/v6/webhooks/events/send" \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer $MANAGEMENT_API_ACCESS_TOKEN" \
  --header "Content-Type: application/json" \
  --data '{
    "type": "CONSUMER",
    "client_id": "'"$CLIENT_ID"'",
    "webhook_id": "'"$WEBHOOK_ID"'",
    "event": {
      "type": "CONSUMER",
      "client_id": "'"$CLIENT_ID"'",
      "webhook_id": "'"$WEBHOOK_ID"'",
      "event_id": "sample00000000000001",
      "root_event_id": "0",
      "cause_event_id": "0",
      "cause_event_sequence": 1,
      "device_profile_id": "'"$TEST_PERSON_ID"'",
      "campaign_id": "'"$CAMPAIGN_ID"'",
      "program_label": "refer-a-friend",
      "journey_name": "everyone",
      "client_domain": "'"$PROGRAM_DOMAIN"'",
      "client_domain_id": "'"$CLIENT_DOMAIN_ID"'",
      "sandbox": "production-production",
      "variant": "en",
      "schema_version": 6,
      "data": {
        "type": "Advocate Rewarded",
        "user": { "email": "sample@example.com", "phone": "+15125550123" },
        "properties": { "funnel": "Advocate", "coupon_code": "SAMPLE20" }
      }
    }
  }'
```

`event.data` is delivered to Attentive as the request body, unchanged. It is the same object the campaign step would have built, so copy the shape from the step's webhook action: a dotted key there, such as `user.phone`, arrives nested.

Use the subscriptions webhook id for a subscribe and the custom events webhook id for `Advocate Rewarded` or `Friend Rewarded`; both ids are on the **Configurations** tab. Each call is a real call to Attentive and creates a real subscriber or event, so use test identities.

`device_profile_id` has to be an existing profile in the account — the dispatcher looks it up — so use a test person's id rather than a placeholder. The other ids in the envelope are free-form.

### Confirm the Sample Arrived

[//]: # "How do I check that a sample event reached Attentive?"

The `200` from the send endpoint means Extole queued the event, not that Attentive accepted it. Attentive's own reply is recorded in the **Webhook Dispatch Result Events** report, which carries the exact request body sent, the response status code and the response body for every dispatch:

```bash
curl --request POST "$EXTOLE_API_HOST/v4/reports" \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer $MANAGEMENT_API_ACCESS_TOKEN" \
  --header "Content-Type: application/json" \
  --data '{
    "report_type": "WEBHOOK_DISPATCH_RESULT_EVENTS",
    "formats": ["JSON"],
    "parameters": { "time_range": "LAST_DAY", "webhook_id": "'"$WEBHOOK_ID"'", "limit": "50" }
  }'
```

That report is built from stored event data rather than read live, so a dispatch from the last few minutes will not be in it yet. To see a sample immediately, look in Attentive — a subscribe appears against the sign-up unit, and a custom event appears in the journey builder's event list, which is what makes its properties available as macros.

<Note>
  **A subscribe still needs a Sign Up Source ID**

  Attentive requires every opt-in request to carry either a sign-up source id, or both a locale and a subscription type. Extole's subscription payload sends the Sign Up Source ID from the **Configurations** tab, so while that field is empty a sample subscribe is rejected. The two reward events do not use it.
</Note>

## Using this Integration

[//]: # "How do I use the Extole integration with Attentive?"

### Send Referral Messages Using Custom Journeys

After the integration is connected, you can return to your Attentive account to create your journeys. By default, this integration includes journeys for Advocate Reward Earned and Friend Reward Earned custom events, which will trigger text messages with coupon codes to advocates and friends as they earn rewards through the referral program. If you’d like additional events or event properties, please work with your Extole customer success manager to configure in your Extole account.

To set up your journeys, select the `Extole - Advocate Rewarded` and `Extole - Friend Rewarded` journeys in the Extole integration app.

## Disconnecting the Integration

[//]: # "How do I disconnect the Extole integration with Attentive?"

If for whatever reason you need to disconnect your Attentive integration, you will need to make sure that the integration is not in use. In the Campaigns tab of the Attentive integration page, deselect any campaigns and be sure to publish your changes. Once there are no campaigns actively using the integration, you should be able to hit the Disconnect button.

<Warning>
**Please Note**


Be sure that you want to disconnect the integration. If you want to reconnect in the future, you will need to restart the integration process.
</Warning>
