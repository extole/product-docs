# Overview

A person clicks the link in their reward email and the Reward Bank opens with no rewards, or with a zero balance. Their rewards are on their profile. The link itself is the cause: it was signed for the person as they were when they earned the reward, and it no longer names them as they are now.

## How a Reward Bank Link Admits a Person

When a person earns a collectible reward, Extole builds a redemption link and stores it on the reward. The link carries a token, and the token names the person by their identity value at that moment, for example their email address. The token expires two months after the reward is earned.

The Reward Bank shows the rewards of the person the token names. So a stored link stops showing the person's rewards in two cases:

- **The person's identity value changed.** If the person changed their email after earning the reward, the token still names the old email. The bank opens for a profile that holds none of their rewards.
- **The token expired.** A link opened more than two months after the reward was earned no longer admits the person.

Changing the person's email does not update links that were already sent. See [How to Change a Person's Email](/guides/rewards-management/reward-fulfillment/how-to-change-a-persons-email).

This token is not the email JWT described in the [Reward Bank Configuration Guide](/technical/platform-integrations/extensions/reward-bank/reward-bank-configuration-guide). That JWT is one your own email builds, with a lifetime you set.

## Why Resending the Reward Email Does Not Help

A reward email sent for a reward uses the link stored on that reward. Resending it delivers the same link, with the same token, so the bank opens the same way.

## Send a Fresh Link

A reward email can sign a new token when it is sent, instead of using a stored link. It does this when two things are true:

- Its **Reward Redemption Button Destination** is a zone name, such as the Reward Bank's redemption center zone, rather than a full URL.
- It is sent without a reward. An email sent for a reward uses that reward's stored link.

The new token names the person by the identity value on their profile when the email is sent. It lasts one day. If the person opens the email after that, send it again.

To send a fresh link:

1. Confirm that the person's profile holds their current email address.
2. Send them a reward email whose **Reward Redemption Button Destination** is the redemption center zone. Send it from a campaign step that runs for one person on an input event, not for a reward. If the program has no such step, add one first.
3. Ask the person to open the new email, and confirm that the Reward Bank shows their rewards.

A fresh link changes no reward. Nothing is earned, counted or paid again.

<Note>
This fix is confirmed for the Reward Bank on a Legacy campaign. For a Reward Bank on a Flow Builder campaign, contact Extole Support.
</Note>
