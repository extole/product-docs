# Overview

Every program template is a tree of the same four parts: roles, journeys, business events, and
rewards. When no template matches your program, you design that tree yourself. This page shows
how to read a program description as a tree, and where to put each count, time window, and limit,
so that your systems send one plain event for each action and Extole evaluates the rest.

[Configuration Concepts](/api-reference/getting-started/configuration-concepts) defines the
parts. Once you have a design,
[Build a Campaign from Components Over REST](/technical/platform-integrations/rest-apis/build-a-campaign-from-components-over-rest)
assembles it with the Management API, and
[How to Add a New Business Event](/guides/flow-campaigns/how-to-add-a-new-business-event)
covers the same work in Flow Builder.

## Read the Program as People, Stages, and Actions

| In the description | In the campaign | How the templates use it |
|---|---|---|
| Each kind of person | A role | `advocate` and `friend` in Refer A Friend, `participant` in Reward for Action |
| Each stage a person moves through | A journey in that role | A marketing journey holds the promotions, and a second journey holds the actions that follow |
| Each action a person takes | A business event in that journey | `shared`, `signed_up`, `converted` |
| Each thing a person earns | A reward on the role that earns it | Advocate Reward on Friend Converted |

Three habits keep the tree readable in Flow Builder and in reports:

- **One business event for each action.** Give an action its own business event even when it
  only counts toward a later condition. Rules read the events that the campaign records.
- **Name the business event after the action.** The component name becomes the event name on
  the person's profile and in reports.
- **Put the business event in the role of the person who acts.** A reward for someone else
  reaches that person's role through a published event, as
  [Count Distinct People Toward Another Person's Reward](#count-distinct-people-toward-another-persons-reward)
  describes.

## Give Each Part What It Needs to Run

The Custom Campaign Template holds one role, one journey, and one business event. Every
template that runs a program adds the following, and a design from components adds them too:

| Part | What it needs | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| Business event | A trigger: the input event, zone, or published event that starts it | [How to Set Up Triggered By](/guides/flow-campaigns/how-to-set-up-triggered-by) |
| Journey | A targeting scenario, which decides who enters the journey | [How to Target a Program](/guides/flow-campaigns/how-to-target-a-program) |
| Reward | The business event that earns it, a reward supplier, and a reward email | [How to Add a New Reward](/guides/flow-campaigns/how-to-add-a-new-reward) |
| Reward | Reward rules that limit how often and to whom it is issued | [How to Set Up Reward Rules](/guides/flow-campaigns/how-to-set-up-reward-rules) |

Give every reward a supplier from the start, even before your fulfillment exists. A custom reward
that Extole fulfills and sends by itself works as a placeholder: a test person earns the reward
and receives the reward email, and you replace the supplier when your fulfillment is ready.
[Build a Campaign from Components Over REST](/technical/platform-integrations/rest-apis/build-a-campaign-from-components-over-rest#use-a-placeholder-supplier-until-fulfillment-is-connected)
has the settings.

## Send Plain Events and Let Extole Evaluate the Conditions

Design the integration so that your systems send one event each time a person does something,
with the identifiers of the people and records involved. Put every condition on those events in
the campaign. A condition in the campaign is visible in Flow Builder and changes without a
release of your systems.

| The program says | Put it in the campaign as |
|---|---|
| "Counts once for each order, account, or membership" | A unique event key on the business event's data. Extole records the business event once for each key value, and the event's actions run once. See [Unique Event Keys](/technical/troubleshooting/duplicate-action-alerts-and-unique-event-keys#unique-event-keys). |
| "Only after the person did something else" | **Has Taken Action** as a trigger rule on the business event. See [Common Trigger Rules](/guides/flow-campaigns/common-trigger-rules). |
| "At least 30 days after joining" | **Minimum Action Age** on that **Has Taken Action** rule. The earlier event counts only when it is at least that old. |
| "In the last 30 days" | **Recent Activity Window** on **Has Taken Action**. |
| "On the third time" or "at least five times" | **Exact Count** or **Minimum Count** on **Has Taken Action**, as a reward rule. |
| "Unless they cancel" | **Has Not Taken Action**, with the event that disqualifies the person. |
| "When someone they brought in does something" | The **Publish Event Data** action on the other person's business event. |
| "Once", or "once every six months" | **Reward Limit**, with **Maximum Count** and **Recent Activity Window**. |
| "Once for each order or group" | **Reward Behavior** set to **Every Unique Action**, with **Unique Reward Data Parameter** set to the field that names the order or group. |
| "After a waiting period" | **Pending Period** on the reward. |
| "Only for some people" | A targeting scenario on the journey, or **Member of Audience** on the reward. |

When a condition fits no row, ask whether a second business event would make it fit. A condition
such as "active for 30 days" becomes two events, the one that starts the clock and the one that
proves the person is still there, joined by **Has Taken Action** with a **Minimum Action Age**.

## Count Distinct People Toward Another Person's Reward

A reward for one person can depend on how many other people did something, such as a reward for
an organizer when three members are active. **Has Taken Action** counts the events on the profile
of the person the reward is for, so each member's event must reach the organizer once:

1. In the member's role, add a business event for the moment a member starts to count. Put the
   conditions on it as trigger rules, so the event is recorded only when the member meets them.
2. Give that business event a unique event key from a field that identifies the member's place
   in the group, such as a membership id. Extole then records the event once for each member,
   however often your systems send it.
3. Add the **Publish Event Data** action to that business event, and set **Target Role** to the
   role that earns the reward. The action runs once for each recorded event.
4. In the earning role, add a business event that is triggered by the published event.
5. On each reward, add **Has Taken Action** as a reward rule. Set **Business Event** to the
   business event from step 4, and set **Exact Count** to the number of people.

The **Person Identification** socket on **Publish Event Data** decides who receives the event.
The action arrives with **Person Identified by Referral Relationship**, which sends the event to
the person's referrer. To send the event to a person that the event data names, replace it with
one of these:

| Person identification | Who receives the event |
|---|---|
| **Person Identified by Identity Key** | The person with the identity key, such as an email, in the event data. |
| **Person Identified By Alternate Person Key** | The person with the person key, such as a partner user ID, in the event data. |
| **Person Identified by Extole Person ID** | The person with the Extole person ID in the event data. |

**Has Taken Action** counts every matching event on the profile in the rule's scope. An organizer
who runs two groups has one count across both.

[How to Set Up Bonus Rewards on a Specific Referral Count](/guides/flow-campaigns/how-to-set-up-bonus-rewards-on-a-specific-referral-count-2nd-3rd-5th-etc)
shows steps 3 to 5 in Flow Builder for an advocate and friends.

## Worked Example

A community program rewards an organizer when three members of the organizer's group are active,
and again at five members. A member is active when the member joined the group and attended a
session at least 30 days later. The organizer can earn each reward once in six months.

Your systems send two events, `group_joined` and `session_attended`. Each carries the member, a
`membership_id`, and the organizer's identifier. The campaign holds everything else:

```text
Role: Member
  Journey: Membership
    Business event: Group Joined          triggered by input event group_joined
    Business event: Member Qualified      triggered by input event session_attended
      Trigger rule:  Has Taken Action     Group Joined, Minimum Action Age 30 days
      Data:          membership_id        the unique event key
      Action:        Publish Event Data   Target Role: Organizer

Role: Organizer
  Journey: Group Activity
    Business event: Active Member Counted triggered by the published event
  Reward: Three Active Members            earned on Active Member Counted
      Reward rule:   Has Taken Action     Active Member Counted, Minimum Count 3,
                                          Recent Activity Window 6 months
      Reward rule:   Reward Limit         this reward's name, Maximum Count 1,
                                          Recent Activity Window 6 months
  Reward: Five Active Members             earned on Active Member Counted
      Reward rule:   Has Taken Action     Active Member Counted, Minimum Count 5,
                                          Recent Activity Window 6 months
      Reward rule:   Reward Limit         this reward's name, Maximum Count 1,
                                          Recent Activity Window 6 months
```

Extole checks that the member joined at least 30 days before the session, records the
qualification once for each membership, counts the members on the organizer's profile, and limits
each reward. Your systems do no counting.

A campaign with more than one reward sets **Applicable Reward Name** on each **Reward Limit**, so
that each limit counts its own reward.
