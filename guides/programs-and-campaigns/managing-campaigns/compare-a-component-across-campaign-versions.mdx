# Overview

The **Component Diff** compares one component of a campaign — an email, a reward, a business event, or any other component — at two campaign versions, setting by setting. Use it to check what an edit or an upgrade changed, or to bring settings from an earlier version back into your draft.

Users with Admin access can open the Component Diff. It is not available to the [User Support role](/guides/user-management/managing-users/user-roles-permissions).

# Open the Component Diff

1. Open the campaign, then open the component you want to compare — for example, select a step in Flow Builder.
2. In the component's settings drawer, click the **Component actions** menu (⋮).
3. Select **Component Diff**.

A link to the Component Diff opens it directly, with the component and both versions already chosen.

# Choose What to Compare

The Component Diff has two sides:

- **Current**: the component you opened. Its settings are the ones you change when you apply.
- **Incoming**: the component you compare it with. Click **Select component**, then choose a component in the campaign or paste a component ID. Usually this is the same component, at another version.

Each side has its own version picker:

- **Published**: the version your customers see. It is unavailable for a campaign that has never been published.
- **Latest draft**: the version with your most recent applied edits.
- **Version history**: the campaign's recent versions, each with its state and date. You can also type a version number into the picker.

To start over with a different incoming component, click **Clear comparison** (×).

# Read the Settings Tab

The **Settings** tab lists only what differs between the two sides. The count under the **Component Diff** title shows how many settings changed.

- **Sockets**: appears first when the component's sockets differ. A socket is where child components are installed — for example, the actions that run after a business event. A socket is listed when it was added or removed, when its child components changed, when they changed order, or when a child component has a new version. The socket rows are for review only.
- **Modified**: settings that exist on both sides with different values.
- **Added**: settings that exist only on the incoming side.
- **Removed**: settings that exist only on the current side.

Use the **All**, **Modified**, **Added**, and **Removed** filters to show one section at a time.

# Bring Settings Into Your Draft

Every setting starts as **Reject**, which keeps the current value. To take the incoming side instead:

1. Click **Accept** on each setting you want. To decide a whole section at once, use its **Accept all** or **Reject all**. The **Reject all** and **Accept all** buttons beside the filters apply to every setting.
2. Click **Apply changes**.

Accepting an added setting adds it to the current component, and accepting a removed setting deletes it. **Apply changes** writes the accepted settings into the current component's draft. Your customers don't see the change until you publish the campaign. See [Making Updates to your Campaigns](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/managing-campaigns/making-updates-to-your-campaigns) for how applying and publishing differ.

# Read the Tree Tab

The **Tree** tab shows the component and its child components, and marks each child that was added, removed, or modified between the two sides. Select a child component to see its own settings diff. To edit a child, hover over it and click **Open in component builder**.

The Tree tab is for review. To bring settings into your draft, use the **Settings** tab.
