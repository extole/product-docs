# Overview

When optimizing your referral program, it’s important to measure how changes to your campaign affect [key metrics on your dashboard](/guides/platform-overview/program-and-campaign-dashboards). To accurately test and compare results, we recommend creating a new campaign whenever you want to try new copy or creative.

# Duplicate a Campaign

1. Go to the [Programs page](/guides/platform-overview/exploring-the-programs-page).
2. Hover over the campaign you want to copy and click the Duplicate icon.

  1. Alternatively, from your Program Dashboard, click the three dots next to the campaign and select Duplicate.

3. A new campaign will be created with the same name.

  1. Go to the Basics tab to edit the Campaign Name and Description so you can easily distinguish it from the original.

# Update Copy and Creative

Your team can make any necessary updates within the Campaign Editor. Need help with the editor? Check out [Using Extole’s Campaign Editor](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/campaign-creation-and-editing/using-extole-s-campaign-editor). After making your changes, click **Apply**.

# Apply and Publish

**Apply** and **Publish Changes** are two different steps, and your customers only see the second one.

- **Apply** saves your edits as a new draft version of the campaign. It is what the Campaign Editor preview shows you, and it changes nothing that your customers see.
- **Publish Changes** makes that draft the version Extole serves. Until you publish, Extole keeps serving the last version you published — and if you have never published this campaign, it serves nothing from it at all.

On the [Programs page](/guides/platform-overview/exploring-the-programs-page), a campaign with edits you have applied but not published shows **Unpublished changes** next to its state, and a campaign that has never been published shows no **Last Published** date.

## Campaigns That Are Not Live Yet

Applying changes to a Not Launched campaign — a new campaign, or a duplicate you are preparing to swap in — does not get it ready to launch. Publish it as well. Make a habit of publishing every time you finish working on a Not Launched campaign.

<Warning>
  **A campaign that has never been published cannot go live, even on a schedule.** Extole works a campaign's state out from its dates every time it reads it, and the check for whether the campaign has ever been published runs first. A start date on a never-published campaign is saved and displayed, but it never takes effect, and nothing tells you it did not work.

  If the campaign does have an earlier published version, the schedule goes live with that older version — not with the changes you applied afterward.
</Warning>

**Go Live** asks you about unpublished changes; a schedule does not. When you choose **Go Live** on a campaign that has a draft, Extole offers you **Publish Changes and Go Live**, or **Go Live with Last Published Version**, which discards the draft. A scheduled go-live gives you neither prompt, so publish before the scheduled time arrives, then confirm afterward that the campaign shows **LIVE**.

# Publish or Set a Campaign Live

To publish changes to a **live** campaign, click **Publish Changes** to update your assets in production.

To launch a **new (not live)** campaign:

1. Click the three dots in the upper right corner and choose **Go Live**.
2. Choose one of the following:

  - **Go Live as a Test**: Divides traffic for A/B testing with your original live campaign.
  - **Go Live as a Burst**: Pauses the original campaign and replaces it with your updated campaign in production.
