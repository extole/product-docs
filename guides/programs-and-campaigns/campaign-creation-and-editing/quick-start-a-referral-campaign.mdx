# Overview

This guide is for launching a referral program as quickly as possible. It covers the settings you must configure in the **Campaign Editor** in My Extole to set up a fully functional, branded referral campaign.

## Configure Overview Settings

In the **Campaign Editor**, fill out the settings in the **Overview** tab. These are overview variables because they live in multiple creatives and cascade throughout your campaign.

## Configure Creative-Specific Settings

After your overview settings are set, fill out the key settings specific to certain creatives.

| Creative | Variables |
| --- | --- |
| Earned Reward Email | Heading |
| Promote Destination | Destination for Clicks on Promotions (if you're not using an Extole-hosted landing page, this should go to your production environment); Extole-Hosted Experience to Trigger on URL Landing |
| Share Experience | Share Experience Image; Mobile Share Experience Image; Share Email Message from Advocate; How it Works Message |
| Stats Email | Email Message |
| Welcome Email | Email Message |
| Friend Landing Experience | Success Panel Heading Text |
| Share Destination | Destination for Clicks on Share Links (where friends land; should direct to your production environment) |
| Share Email | Brand Email Message |
| Terms and Conditions | Terms Copy |
| All other enabled emails | Subject Line |
