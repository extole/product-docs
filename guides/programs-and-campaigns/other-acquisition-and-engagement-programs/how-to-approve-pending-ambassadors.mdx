# Overview

When your Ambassador program uses **manual approval**, applicants submit through the Application
Experience and wait in a pending list until your team reviews them. Approve them from the
**Ambassadors** tab (recommended below). As a secondary option, you can create one-off or batch
approval events: go to **User Support** > **Create Event** for a single approval, or **Tech
Center** > **File Transfer** for multiple. Approving an event on a person's profile
(**Approvals**) reviews that event and does not admit them as an ambassador.

This path applies when your campaign has a pending ambassadors audience (the default for the
**Ambassador with Manual Approval** Flow Builder starter). For when to choose that starter, see
[Ambassador](/product/product-overview/programs/influencer#choosing-a-flow-builder-starter).

# Approve Pending Ambassadors

1. Open your Ambassador program in My Extole, or open the specific ambassador campaign.
2. Select the **Ambassadors** tab.
3. If the table title is **Ambassadors** and you see **Show Pending**, click **Show Pending**.

<Frame caption="On the approved Ambassadors list, click Show Pending to open applications waiting for review.">
  <img
    src="/images/extole/ambassadors-show-pending.png"
    alt="Ambassadors tab showing the approved Ambassadors table with a Show Pending button in the table header."
  />
</Frame>

4. Confirm the table title is **Ambassadors Pending Approval**. Use **Search for ambassadors by
   email** if you need a specific applicant.
5. Select the checkbox next to each applicant you want to review.
6. Click **Approve Selected** to admit them, or **Decline Selected** to reject the application.

<Frame caption="Ambassadors Pending Approval lists applicants waiting for a decision. Approve Selected and Decline Selected stay disabled until you select at least one row.">
  <img
    src="/images/extole/ambassadors-pending-approval.png"
    alt="Ambassadors Pending Approval table with disabled Approve Selected and Decline Selected buttons and a Show Approved toggle."
  />
</Frame>

<Frame caption="Select one or more applicants, then click Approve Selected.">
  <img
    src="/images/extole/ambassadors-approve-selected.png"
    alt="Ambassadors Pending Approval table with two applicants selected and Approve Selected enabled."
  />
</Frame>

After you approve an applicant, they move out of the pending list into the approved Ambassadors
list and receive the welcome messaging your campaign is configured to send. Click **Show Approved**
to return to the approved list.

<Tip>
  For Manual Approval programs, the Ambassadors tab often opens on **Ambassadors Pending Approval**
  already. In that case you can skip **Show Pending** and start at step 4.
</Tip>

# If You Do Not See Show Pending

**Show Pending** appears only when the campaign has a pending ambassadors audience configured.
Campaigns that use **Ambassador with No Application** or **Ambassador with Auto Approval** do not
show a pending-approval queue on this tab.

If applicants are waiting and you still do not see **Show Pending** or **Ambassadors Pending
Approval**, confirm you opened the correct ambassador campaign (use the campaign dropdown next to
**Ambassadors** when the program has more than one), then contact your Extole team.

# Secondary Option: Create Approval Events

If you prefer not to use the Ambassadors tab, you can approve applicants by creating approval
events:

1. For one person, go to **User Support** > **Create Event**, select the Ambassador program and
   campaign, choose the approval event your campaign uses, enter the applicant's email, and submit.
2. For multiple people, upload a file through **Tech Center** > **File Transfer**.

Once the approval event is created, the applicant receives the Application Approval Email and moves
into the approved Ambassadors list.

<Warning>
  The edit or remove control on an Ambassadors row removes someone from a list. It does not approve
  their application.
</Warning>
