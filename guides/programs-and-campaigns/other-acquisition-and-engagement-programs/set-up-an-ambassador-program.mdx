## Turn People with Big Audiences into a Revenue Channel

Some of your customers and partners have something you can't buy: an audience that trusts them. An [Ambassador program](/product/product-overview/programs/influencer) turns that reach into revenue. Each ambassador gets a personal share link or code and a dashboard to watch their results, and you reward them for the sales they drive with offers that are richer than your everyday referral program. A fitness instructor with a loyal class, a creator with a following, a partner whose customers overlap with yours, or a top customer who talks about you everywhere can each bring in new customers at scale, and you can track and measure every one of them.

Ambassador programs need little technical work beyond what you already have for Refer a Friend. This guide walks through the setup in order:

1. Choose how people join.
2. Decide who your ambassadors will be.
3. Create the program in My Extole.
4. Set up the assets and emails.
5. Set rewards, including Reward Bank.
6. Give ambassadors their stats.
7. Test and launch.

Two companion guides go deeper where you'll need them. [How to Approve Pending Ambassadors](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/other-acquisition-and-engagement-programs/how-to-approve-pending-ambassadors) covers manual approval, and the [Ambassador and Influencer Assets and Emails Reference](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/other-acquisition-and-engagement-programs/ambassador-assets-and-emails) lists what to configure for each way of joining.

## Step 1: Choose How People Join

When you create an Ambassador program from the template library, you choose one of three pre-configured Flow Builder starters. Each one comes with the approval path, audiences, events, triggers, and rules that match it, so choose by how you plan to add ambassadors, not by which name sounds closest to your program.

- **Ambassador with No Application.** Use this when you predetermine the ambassadors that are eligible. There is no application. You invite ambassadors one at a time or in batches, they receive the Ambassador Welcome email with a personal share link or code, and they share from their dashboard. It suits partners, VIP customers, super advocates you've identified in your Refer a Friend program, or a roster you keep outside Extole.
- **Ambassador with Auto Approval.** Use this when you promote the program broadly and anyone who applies can join. People apply through the Application Experience in the Ambassador Portal and are approved automatically. You collect more information about them than a normal referral would, and you manage them as their own audience. You can require something, such as a minimum number of followers, by adding a field to the application. Extole goes by what the applicant enters and does not verify it. It suits programs you expect to grow without reviewing every application.
- **Ambassador with Manual Approval.** Use this when you promote the program broadly but a person on your team must approve each applicant. Applicants submit through the same Application Experience, and pending applicants wait on the program's **Ambassadors** tab for review before they get the welcome email and sharing access. It suits programs where brand fit or compliance review matters more than automated criteria.

A quick way to decide: if you already know who your ambassadors are, choose No Application. If you'd welcome anyone who applies, choose Auto Approval. If someone on your team needs to review each applicant, choose Manual Approval.

## Step 2: Decide Who Your Ambassadors Will Be

Extole builds the ambassador audience for you. With Auto Approval and Manual Approval, people who apply are added automatically, and a Manual Approval program holds applicants in a pending audience until you approve them. With No Application you add ambassadors yourself, so you don't need to prepare a list in advance. You can see and manage everyone in [My Audiences](/product/product-overview/people/audience-discovery).

What's left for you is deciding who you want, and who to ask. A few ideas:

- **Your best advocates.** Customers who already share a lot in your Refer a Friend program. The Influencer Share Links Performance and Top Share Links reports, in the **Segments & People** section of **Reports**, show who they are. [Run Targeted Super Advocate Programs](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/other-acquisition-and-engagement-programs/run-targeted-super-advocate-programs) shows how.
- **VIP and long-time customers.** Your top spenders and most loyal customers already talk about you.
- **Creators and influencers.** People with an audience who already mention or buy from you, and the ones you'd like to recruit.
- **Partners and affiliates.** Businesses and publishers whose audience overlaps with yours.
- **Professionals who recommend you.** Instructors, coaches, and doctors whose clients and patients trust their advice.
- **Sales agents and other third parties.** Anyone who meets your customers and can share a link on your behalf.

Then decide how you'll reach them:

1. **Invite them directly** if you chose No Application. Step 7 covers how.
2. **Promote the program** if you chose Auto Approval or Manual Approval. Put it where your target group already pays attention: your site, your email list, and your social channels. To email a specific group, [upload them as an audience](/guides/audiences-and-segmentation/my-audiences/upload-and-email-an-audience#create-audiences) and send them the invitation.
3. **Limit who can see or enter the program, if you need to.** The program's [targeting](/guides/flow-campaigns/how-to-target-a-program) can include or exclude audiences from My Audiences.

## Step 3: Create the Program

A new program lets you give ambassadors their own offer, creative, and rewards, and track them separately. This is the right move when you're [targeting a different audience with a unique flow or reward structure](/guides/platform-overview/programs-vs-campaigns).

1. Go to the [Programs page](https://my.extole.com/programs) in My Extole.
2. Click **+ New Program**.
3. Choose the Ambassador template for the starter you picked in Step 1: No Application, Auto Approval, or Manual Approval. If you don't see the one you need, ask your CSM.
4. Enter a name for your program.
5. Confirm to create it.

Extole opens the new program as a copy of the template, in draft. It includes one campaign and its flow, the rewards that campaign issues, and the eligibility and quality rules that decide who gets rewarded. Nothing goes live until you launch it.

The template's reward values and rules are placeholders, not recommendations for your goal, so plan to update them in the steps below. [Creating a Program](/guides/platform-overview/creating-a-program) has more detail.

## Step 4: Set Up the Assets and Emails

An Ambassador program has more pieces than a Refer a Friend program, and which ones you need depends on how people join. Start with the assets every program has.

On the ambassador side:

- **Microsite.** The portal ambassadors use to apply, if you have an application, and later to see their dashboard. It controls the look behind the Application Experience, the Share Experience, and the stats.
- **Application Experience.** The entry point for Auto Approval and Manual Approval programs. The person enters their email, confirms through an Authentication Email, and then submits their application. This is where you set the application fields and the approval method.
- **Share Experience.** The sharing options in the ambassador's dashboard. Many ambassadors copy their personal link or code, but the dashboard lets them share on the web or natively from a phone.
- **Influencer Stats.** The heart of the dashboard (see Step 6).

On the friend and follower side:

- **Landing Experience.** What a person sees after opening an ambassador's link. It shows the ambassador's name, delivers the reward, and invites the visitor to shop or sign up.
- **Friend Earned Reward email.** Needed if you capture email addresses and reward friends.

To edit these, open your campaign and go to the **Assets** tab (or **Edit Creatives**), or open them from the Flow Builder.

Then set up the emails. The path you chose changes which ones you need:

- **No Application:** the welcome email you send when you invite someone, and the reminder that follows if they haven't opened it.
- **Auto Approval:** the Authentication Email, Application Received, and Application Approved emails, with the welcome email sent right after the application.
- **Manual Approval:** the same, plus Application Declined, and the welcome email is sent only after you approve.
- **Every path:** the Stats, Earned Reward, and Has Not Influenced emails.

The [Ambassador and Influencer Assets and Emails Reference](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/other-acquisition-and-engagement-programs/ambassador-assets-and-emails) lists every asset and email, when each is sent, and which path uses it.

## Step 5: Set Rewards, Including Reward Bank

Ambassadors earn a reward each time a friend or follower uses their link or code to complete a purchase. Make these rewards richer than your everyday referral reward, since that is part of what makes being an ambassador feel special. Three options work well:

- **A percentage of the sale.** The ambassador earns a share of what their friends spend, such as 5% of the cart value. Bigger orders earn more, and your cost stays a fixed share of the sales they bring in. Reward Bank can pay a percentage of a purchase (see below).
- **A higher discount.** Give ambassadors, or the friends they bring, a richer discount than your standard referral offer. For example, an ambassador might earn 20% off where a regular advocate earns 15%.
- **A gift card.** A gift card is simple to understand and works well for ambassadors who aren't customers, such as partners and professionals.

Whichever you choose, consider adding a bonus once an ambassador's friends have spent a set amount, such as \$250.

To set the reward in your campaign:

1. Open the campaign's flow in the Flow Builder.
2. Find the business event that should trigger the reward, such as the friend's purchase, and add a reward to it. [How to Add a New Reward](/guides/flow-campaigns/how-to-add-a-new-reward) shows the steps, and [How to Set Up a Reward](/guides/flow-campaigns/how-to-set-up-a-reward) explains the default rewards that come with a flow.
3. Choose the reward type and value, such as a coupon, a gift card, or a payout through [PayPal](/guides/rewards-management/sending-rewards-with-tremendous).
4. Check the reward rules and limits, and update any template values that don't fit your program. See [Configuring the Rules of Your Program](/guides/rewards-management/rules-configuration/configuring-the-rules-of-your-program).

### Reward Bank for Ambassadors

[Reward Bank](/technical/platform-integrations/extensions/reward-bank) lets people collect rewards over time and redeem them when they choose, through a personal rewards dashboard and a redemption center. It is well suited to ambassadors, because they tend to earn many smaller rewards, and it spreads the cost of rewards over time. You can also use it for rewards that scale with sales: a collectible reward can use a dynamic value to pay a percentage, such as 10% of a purchase, with a minimum and a maximum per transaction.

To set it up, follow the [Reward Bank Configuration Guide](/technical/platform-integrations/extensions/reward-bank/reward-bank-configuration-guide). In short:

1. Create the reward suppliers that people will redeem into, with a dynamic value, on the **Account Rewards** page.
2. Install the Reward Bank extension from **Partners** > **Extensions**.
3. Add your collectible rewards, the rewards earned for each action, and set when they expire if you want them to.
4. Choose the redemption options, such as gift cards.
5. Set the redemption minimum and maximum, and the redemption ratio (how many points equal one dollar).

When you add Reward Bank to a flow, make sure the other pieces that handle rewards, such as webhooks and controllers, are updated to work with it. You may also need help from Support to point Earned Reward emails to the Reward Bank, so involve your CSM early.

## Step 6: Give Ambassadors Their Stats

A personal dashboard is a big part of what makes the program feel different from referral. Ambassadors can see their progress and rewards in real time, which keeps them motivated and drives more sharing. The [Advocate Stats Dashboard](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/add-an-advocate-stats-dashboard) is the page to set up.

The dashboard shows:

- **Referrals, earned rewards, and earned value.** Referrals counts the times they explicitly shared. Earned rewards is how many rewards they've received, and earned value is the total value of those rewards.
- **Your rewards.** A feed of each reward, with its type, value, and source.
- **Your activity.** A feed of recent shares and the channels they used.

You can show the dashboard in three places:

1. **On the sharing microsite.** This is the out-of-the-box option. In the Campaign Editor, go to the microsite section and turn on **Display Embedded Stats**. If you don't see the option, ask your CSM to upgrade the campaign.
2. **Embedded behind login on your own website.** Use this when ambassadors are already signed in on your site. Your system passes a signed token so Extole knows who they are, and you add the stats zone to the page. Your Extole support team enables the zone.
3. **Through the Extole APIs.** Use this for a fully custom experience, such as inside your own app.

The emails keep ambassadors looped in too. The Stats Email shows approved ambassadors their referrals, rewards, and earned value, and the Earned Reward Email goes out each time they earn.

For your own view, open the program page in My Extole. The program dashboard shows how many people are taking part, the conversions, and the revenue driven. The ambassador table lower on the page breaks it down by person, sorted by revenue by default, and you can search, sort, and edit from there.

## Step 7: Test, Launch, and Bring In Ambassadors

Your program stays in draft until you launch it, so you have time to test.

1. **Test the ambassador's path.** Go through it as an ambassador would: apply, if your path has an application, receive the emails, open the dashboard, and copy the link. Then follow the link as a friend and complete the action you reward.
2. **Check the approval step.** For Manual Approval, approve a test applicant from the **Ambassadors** tab and confirm the welcome email arrives.
3. **Work through the checklist.** The [Go-Live QA Checklist](/guides/platform-overview/go-live-qa-checklist) covers what to confirm before you go live.
4. **Launch** the program from the Programs page. The campaign must be published for ambassadors to receive invite emails.

### Bring In Your First Ambassadors

- **No Application.** On your program page in My Extole, find the **Influencers** section and click **+ New Influencer**. Enter the person's details to create their personal share link, then send the invite email when you create them or later by editing them and clicking **Send Invite Email**. To add many at once, upload a file through File Transfer, which your Implementation Manager or CSM can help with. Invited ambassadors who don't open the first email get a reminder after seven days.
- **Auto Approval and Manual Approval.** Promote the program to your audience and send people to the Application Experience. Keep the invitation clean and concise, so it's clear how to take part.

### Approve Applicants (Manual Approval)

Open your program in My Extole, go to the **Ambassadors** tab, and click **Show Pending** if the list isn't already showing applicants. Select the people you want, then click **Approve Selected** or **Decline Selected**. Approved applicants move to the Ambassadors list and receive the welcome email. See [How to Approve Pending Ambassadors](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/other-acquisition-and-engagement-programs/how-to-approve-pending-ambassadors) for the full steps, including approving in bulk with a file.

### Keep Them Engaged

Define what a successful ambassador looks like, keep your top ambassadors on rich offers, and send regular nurture emails. The Has Not Influenced email reminds ambassadors who haven't shared in a while, after seven days by default.
