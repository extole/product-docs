## Assets and Emails by Approval Type

An Ambassador program has more assets and emails than most programs, and the ones you need depend on the starter you chose. This reference lists them all, so you can see what to set up. For the order of setup, see [Ambassador and Influencer Program: Step-by-Step Setup](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/other-acquisition-and-engagement-programs/set-up-an-ambassador-program).

| Asset or email | No Application | Auto Approval | Manual Approval |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Microsite | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Application Experience | No | Yes | Yes |
| Share Experience | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Influencer Stats | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Friend Landing Experience | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Ambassador Welcome email | Yes, when you invite | Yes, right after applying | Yes, after you approve |
| Remind email | Yes | No | No |
| Authentication Email | No | Yes | Yes |
| Application Received email | No | Yes | Yes |
| Application Approved email | No | Yes | Yes |
| Application Declined email | No | No | Yes |
| Stats, Earned Reward, and Has Not Influenced emails | Yes | Yes | Yes |

## Ambassador-Side Assets

These are what ambassadors see. You can edit them from your campaign's **Assets** tab or **Edit Creatives**, or from the Flow Builder. [Editing Your Advocate Microsite and Share Experience](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/editing-the-advocate-microsite-and-share-experience) shows where they are.

- **Microsite.** The portal ambassadors use to apply, if your program has an application, and later to reach their dashboard. It controls the look behind the Application Experience, the Share Experience, and the stats.
- **Application Experience.** The entry point for Auto Approval and Manual Approval programs. First the person enters their email, which triggers the Authentication Email. After they click the link in that email, they can submit their application. This asset controls the fields in both steps and the approval method.
- **Share Experience.** The sharing options in the ambassador's dashboard, such as sharing on the web or natively from a phone across social channels, SMS, or email. Many ambassadors skip it and copy their personal link or code, which they can paste straight into a social bio.
- **Influencer Stats.** The ambassador's dashboard. It shows their profile picture, referrals, earned rewards, and earned value, a feed of their rewards with type, value, and source, and a feed of their activity with the channels used. See the [Advocate Stats Dashboard](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/add-an-advocate-stats-dashboard) guide for where you can show it.

## Friend and Follower Assets

This is the other half of the program: what people see when they use an ambassador's link or code.

- **Landing Experience.** The page a friend or follower reaches after opening an ambassador's link. It shows the ambassador's name and profile image, delivers any reward they've earned, and encourages the visitor to shop or sign up.
- **Friend Earned Reward email.** If you capture email addresses on the landing page and reward friends, enable this email and set up its content.

## Emails

Emails to ambassadors go out in roughly this order. Check the table at the top of this page for which ones your path uses.

| Email | When it's sent | What it does |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Ambassador Welcome (also called the New Influencer email) | When you invite someone (No Application), right after they apply (Auto Approval), or after you approve them (Manual Approval) | Welcomes them and includes their personal share link or code and a link to their dashboard |
| Remind | Automatically, if an invited ambassador hasn't opened the first email after seven days | Nudges them to open the invitation |
| Authentication Email | When someone enters their email in the first part of the Application Experience | Verifies their identity and includes the link to apply |
| Application Received | After someone submits their application | Confirms it and summarizes the fields they entered |
| Application Approved | Right after applying (Auto Approval), or after you approve (Manual Approval) | Tells them they're in and links to their stats page |
| Application Declined | When you decline an applicant (Manual Approval) | By default, links to your standard Refer a Friend program and encourages them to take part |
| Stats | To approved ambassadors | Shows referrals, rewards earned, and earned value |
| Earned Reward | Each time an ambassador earns a reward | Gives the reward value and how to redeem it, with a prompt to keep sharing |
| Has Not Influenced | Scheduled, after a set number of inactive days (seven by default), or triggered for an audience that automatically excludes active ambassadors | Reminds ambassadors who haven't shared |

To review applicants for a Manual Approval program, see [How to Approve Pending Ambassadors](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/other-acquisition-and-engagement-programs/how-to-approve-pending-ambassadors).
