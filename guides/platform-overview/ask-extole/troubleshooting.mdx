## Overview

Ask Extole is the assistant in the left navigation of My Extole. It answers questions about your account and can change drafts, or publish and issue rewards, when the conversation's access mode allows it. This page is what to do when it is missing, a chat will not load, a reply fails, or a change is refused.

For what Ask Extole is for, see [Ask Extole](/product/ask-extole/index). For access modes and data handling, see [Access Modes and Data](/product/ask-extole/advanced).

## Open Ask Extole

1. In the left navigation, click **Ask Extole**.
2. Click **New chat**, or choose a thread under **Recent chats**.
3. Click **All chats** for the full list.

**New chat** opens the panel beside the page you are on. The same thread is on its own page at [my.extole.com/chat](https://my.extole.com/chat). A link in a reply that stays inside My Extole keeps the panel open on the destination page.

Press Enter to send. Press Shift+Enter for a new line. You can write the next message while a reply is still running. It shows as **Queued** and sends when the current turn finishes.

A new chat links to the [AI Terms](https://my.extole.com/chat/terms). Read them before you rely on a reply.

## Ask Extole Is Missing

Ask Extole is not turned on for every account. If the left navigation has no **Ask Extole** entry, contact your Extole team. Refreshing the page does not add it.

Cate, the pink chat icon, is a separate support chatbot. Opening Cate does not open Ask Extole.

## A Chat List or a Thread Will Not Load

When **All chats** cannot load, the page says **Chats could not be loaded**. Click **Try again**.

When a single thread keeps spinning, go back to **Ask Extole** and open it again from **Recent chats**. The thread is stored with your sign-in, so it is still there after a refresh.

## A Reply Fails or Stalls

An error appears in the thread. Send the message again.

If the thread says **The assistant is taking longer than expected. Check back in this conversation shortly or try sending your message again.**, leave the thread and open it again from **Recent chats**. A long lookup can finish after that notice. Sending the same message again starts a new turn.

A reply that looked up reports, participants, or configuration often takes longer than a short explanation. Wait for the turn to finish before you judge the answer.

## A Change Is Refused

Publishing, issuing a reward, and account-level changes require **Full access**. Creating and editing drafts require **Build**. A new conversation starts in **Build**.

When the request needs a higher mode, Ask Extole stops that step before it runs and shows a card with **Switch to Full access**.

1. Click **Switch to Full access**. The refused step does not run on its own.
2. Send the request again.

You can also set the mode from the control on the message box before you send. Your My Extole role still applies. **Full access** does not let you do something your role cannot do.

<Warning>
A publish or an issued reward in **Full access** is your action. Read the draft before you tell Ask Extole to publish it.
</Warning>

## A Number Does Not Match Your Reports

1. Open the report card in the reply, or run the same report yourself for the same dates.
2. Check that the question named the program, the side of the journey (advocate or friend), and the period you meant.
3. If the record on screen matters, confirm it is included in the conversation. If it is not, name the program, person, or report in the question.
4. On the reply, click the thumbs-down control and add a reason, or click **File a support ticket**.

To see which lookups ran, open **Conversation actions** and turn on **Developer mode**. Turn it off again for an ordinary thread. **Developer mode** does not change what Ask Extole is allowed to do.

## A Chat Is Getting Long

After you have sent 15 messages in one chat, a banner says **This chat is getting long** and offers **New chat**. Click **New chat** for the next task. Click **Dismiss** to keep working in this thread. You can open the old thread again from **Recent chats** or **All chats**.

There is no delete or rename. The list also has no search yet.

## What Not to Paste

Never paste an access token, a password, or another credential into the message box. Ask Extole already uses your My Extole session. A token in the thread is stored with the conversation.

Do not send another client's data, or consumer personal information your program does not already need.
