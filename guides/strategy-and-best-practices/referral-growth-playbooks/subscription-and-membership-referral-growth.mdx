# Overview

Use this playbook when value is delivered through a recurring subscription, membership, replenishment model, paid plan, or ongoing service. The qualifying journey may include a trial, first payment, paid conversion, renewal, or required period of active service.

Diagnose the program with the shared framework in [Referral Program Growth Playbooks by Industry](/guides/strategy-and-best-practices/referral-growth-playbooks/index) before choosing actions.

## Highest-Leverage Subscription Placements

### Product and Account Experience

- Place a persistent referral entry on the account page, dashboard, plan page, or member menu.
- Trigger a referral invitation after activation, first value, or successful setup rather than immediately after signup.
- Add a confirmation-page invitation after a paid subscription or membership purchase.
- Use the login page or authenticated home screen for customers with established engagement.
- Integrate sharing into the product or app when an external landing page interrupts the experience.

### Mobile App

- Add a home tile and account-menu entry.
- Use push notifications when a new offer launches and at a measured evergreen cadence.
- Test an interstitial after repeat engagement for eligible customers who have not shared.
- Deep link friends to the correct trial, plan, or membership screen.

### Lifecycle Messaging

- Add referral modules to subscription confirmation, activation, usage milestone, renewal, and satisfaction messages.
- Send dedicated email or SMS to engaged members, not every contact indiscriminately.
- Time asks after the customer has experienced value.
- Use reminders for friends who started a trial or signup but did not complete paid conversion.

## The Subscription Advocate Experience

- Explain whether the reward is earned at trial, paid conversion, first payment, or after an active-service period.
- Use benefits that match the relationship, such as a free period, account credit, premium feature, points, or partner benefit.
- Show progress when the reward is delayed by a qualification period.
- Test an embedded share flow and keep it easy to complete on mobile.
- Give advocates a personal link or code that remains easy to find in the account.

## The Subscription Friend Journey

- Send the friend directly to the relevant plan, trial, or product page.
- Keep the referral benefit visible through signup and payment.
- Explain trial length, renewal terms, eligibility, and when the benefit applies.
- Separate trial starts from paid conversions in reporting.
- Use reminders before trial expiration or incomplete paid conversion when permitted.
- Avoid asking for information the customer must enter again during account creation.

## Subscription Reward and Campaign Ideas

- Free weeks or months for the advocate, the friend, or both.
- Account credit or plan upgrades.
- Partner offers that add perceived value without discounting the core subscription.
- Temporary reward bursts around launches or renewal seasons.
- Tiered rewards for multiple paid subscribers.
- Reward campaigns tied to renewal, auto-renew enrollment, product usage, or other retention behaviors.
- Sweepstakes for audience growth when the qualification cycle is long.

See [Referral Program Patterns and Campaign Ideas](/guides/strategy-and-best-practices/referral-growth-playbooks/referral-program-patterns) for the full pattern library.

## Subscription Audience Expansion

Prioritize:

- Recently activated customers who reached a meaningful product milestone.
- Long-tenured or highly engaged subscribers.
- Customers with high satisfaction or repeat usage.
- Past advocates and super advocates.
- Customers on specific plans, products, or service tiers.
- Employees, creators, experts, or partners with a trusted audience.

## Subscription Measurement

Track:

- Referral participation by lifecycle moment and plan.
- Trial starts, paid subscriptions, first-period revenue, and renewal.
- Trial-to-paid conversion and time to paid conversion.
- Active-service duration required for qualification.
- Churn, retention, and lifetime value of referred subscribers compared with other acquisition sources.
- Reward cost, account credit usage, and cost per paid subscriber.

## Subscription 30-60-90 Day Plan

### Days 1-30: Define the Qualified Outcome

- Document the exact trial, payment, and active-service events.
- Separate trial and paid conversion in reporting.
- Add account, confirmation, and lifecycle placements.
- Audit mobile and app journeys.
- Clarify reward timing and terms.

### Days 31-60: Improve Conversion Continuity

- Test direct-to-plan or direct-to-trial landing pages.
- Remove duplicate form steps.
- Add incomplete-trial or incomplete-payment reminders.
- Test a free-period, account-credit, or partner benefit.
- Review performance by plan and customer tenure.

### Days 61-90: Build a Lifecycle Growth Loop

- Trigger referral asks after meaningful usage or satisfaction events.
- Launch a targeted campaign for highly engaged members.
- Add a retention-oriented reward campaign.
- Compare retention and lifetime value of referred subscribers with other sources.

## Common Subscription Pitfalls

- Rewarding a trial start when the business goal is a paid subscriber, without accounting for quality.
- Asking for referrals before the subscriber experiences value.
- Hiding delayed qualification or active-service requirements.
- Sending all friends to a generic pricing page.
- Measuring first payment without monitoring retention.
