# Overview

Use this playbook for cruises, travel bookings, hospitality, tours, and other experiences where the journey may include a booking, deposit, final payment, travel-advisor interaction, onboard or on-property experience, and post-trip relationship.

Diagnose the program with the shared framework in [Referral Program Growth Playbooks by Industry](/guides/strategy-and-best-practices/referral-growth-playbooks/index) before choosing actions.

## Highest-Leverage Travel Placements

### Booking Journey

- Present a referral invitation after booking confirmation, not before the booking is secure.
- Keep an always-on link in the traveler account, booking-management area, or mobile menu.
- Add referral modules to deposit confirmation, payment reminders, final-payment confirmation, and pre-trip messages where they do not distract from required actions.
- Use a contextual invitation after a high-satisfaction service interaction.

### App and Authenticated Experience

- Add a home tile or account-menu entry.
- Use push notifications for campaign launches and relevant travel moments.
- Test an interstitial after repeat engagement for travelers who have not shared.
- Keep the share experience integrated when leaving the app would disrupt itinerary planning.

### Onboard, On-Property, and Post-Trip

- Use posters, room materials, displays, QR codes, or digital signage at high-satisfaction moments.
- Give advisors, planners, or agents unique links when they participate in the referral journey.
- Add take-home cards or post-trip email and survey placements.
- Trigger next-trip or refer-a-travel-companion campaigns soon after a positive experience.

## The Travel Advocate Experience

- Let the traveler share an itinerary, sailing, destination, or general offer without revealing private booking details.
- Explain whether the reward is earned at booking, deposit, final payment, completed travel, or after a cancellation window.
- Use tier progress or status communication when reward qualification is delayed.
- Support QR codes and native mobile sharing.
- Keep the personal link or code available in booking management.

## The Travel Friend Journey

- Send the friend to the relevant itinerary, destination, property, or offer.
- Preserve the referral through advisor, call-center, and online booking paths.
- Separate booking, deposit, final payment, cancellation, and completed-travel milestones.
- Reduce unnecessary fields before the friend reaches the standard booking journey.
- Explain date, cabin or room, fare, eligibility, and reward restrictions before checkout.
- Use reminders for started but incomplete bookings when permitted.

## Travel Reward and Campaign Ideas

- Travel credit, onboard credit, gift card, upgrade, or partner benefit.
- Tiered rewards for booking, final payment, and completed travel.
- Reward bursts around launches, shoulder periods, or selected itineraries.
- Advisor or planner ambassador programs.
- Sweepstakes for audience growth or destination interest, subject to legal review.
- Post-trip reward campaigns for booking the next trip within a defined window.
- Rewards for high-value add-ons or other approved travel actions.

See [Referral Program Patterns and Campaign Ideas](/guides/strategy-and-best-practices/referral-growth-playbooks/referral-program-patterns) for the full pattern library.

## Travel Audience Expansion

Prioritize:

- Recently booked travelers during peak anticipation.
- Recently returned travelers after a positive trip.
- Repeat guests and loyalty members.
- Past advocates and super advocates.
- Travel advisors, planners, call-center staff, and onboard teams.
- Travelers associated with a destination, itinerary, departure market, or property.
- Group organizers and customers traveling for celebrations.

## Travel Measurement

Track:

- Shares by booking, app, email, SMS, onboard, post-trip, and advisor source.
- Friend itinerary views and booking starts.
- Bookings, deposits, final payments, cancellations, and completed travel.
- Booking value, add-on value, and net revenue after cancellations.
- Time from referral to booking and final payment.
- Advisor, itinerary, destination, property, and channel attribution.
- Reward liability and cost per net qualified booking.

## Travel 30-60-90 Day Plan

### Days 1-30: Connect Booking Milestones

- Define booking, deposit, final payment, cancellation, and completed-travel events.
- Add booking-confirmation, account, and post-trip placements.
- Audit referral persistence across online, advisor, and call-center bookings.
- Clarify reward timing and cancellation rules.
- Add source reporting for advisor and onboard channels.

### Days 31-60: Improve Itinerary Relevance

- Test generic destinations against itinerary-specific landing pages.
- Add reminder flows for incomplete bookings.
- Simplify the pre-booking share experience.
- Pilot onboard or on-property QR placements.
- Compare performance by travel moment and itinerary.

### Days 61-90: Expand High-Satisfaction Audiences

- Launch a post-trip or next-trip campaign.
- Pilot an advisor or planner ambassador program.
- Test a tiered reward, reward burst, or sweepstakes.
- Compare net booking quality after cancellations and final payment.

## Common Travel Pitfalls

- Rewarding a booking without accounting for cancellation or final payment.
- Losing referral attribution when a traveler calls an advisor or call center.
- Asking for referrals before the customer has confidence in the experience.
- Sending every friend to a generic homepage.
- Ignoring onboard, on-property, and post-trip advocacy moments.
