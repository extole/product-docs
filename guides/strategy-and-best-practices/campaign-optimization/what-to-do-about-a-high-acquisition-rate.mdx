# Overview

A high acquisition rate can come from several factors, such as affiliate or influencer-style campaigns where many transactors come from a single advocate or share.

Acquisition rate is calculated as (transactors ÷ advocates) × 100. A "transactor" is every distinct person who transacts in the period — a download, booking, purchase, or registration, depending on your program. Each person is counted once, so having two transaction events (for example, registration and purchase) does not affect your acquisition rate.

## Benchmark

A high acquisition rate is over 30.

## How to Investigate a High Acquisition Rate

Start your investigation if there are share links attributed to a high number of transactors. Pull the [Successful Advocates report](/guides/dashboards-and-reporting/report-types/recommended-reports/successful-advocates-report) from the Segments & People reports in the **Reports** tab of My Extole to identify advocates who drove a high volume of transactions.

Once you know how many transactors are attributed to specific advocates, subtract that from the total transactors for the period, then compare with the advocates in that period to recalculate a more accurate acquisition rate.

Once you have identified these people, consider whether your reward and reward limits are set up appropriately. You might also segment these people into an Ambassador program with a different reward structure.
