# Overview

A manual reward is a reward you create yourself from a participant's profile, outside your campaign's normal rewarding logic. It is the right answer when someone earned a reward your campaign could not produce on its own, and the wrong answer when the reward already exists or when a reward the campaign already created only needs approving.

Reach for a manual reward when:

- A reward failed and cannot be approved, because the campaign was stopped, the reward was deleted, or required data is missing.
- The event that should have triggered the reward never reached Extole and cannot be recreated.
- An escalation needs a fast resolution.
- You have decided to reward someone outside your campaign's normal criteria.

<Warning>
**Issue a manual reward last, not first.**

A manual reward is created outside your campaign's rules, so it can produce a duplicate payout, inconsistent reporting, and a reward that breaks a limit you set deliberately — one reward per person, one per referral relationship, or an annual reward value cap. Work through the checks below before you create one.
</Warning>

<Info>
**This page covers campaigns built with Flow Builder.** Look for the flow icon next to the campaign name on the Campaigns page.

In a Legacy campaign you issue the reward through the event it hangs off instead, by creating or approving that event. See [How to Manually Create a Referral or other Event](/guides/rewards-management/reward-fulfillment/how-to-manually-create-a-referral-or-other-event). If you aren't sure which kind of campaign you have, ask your CSM.
</Info>

# Decide Whether a Manual Reward Is the Right Fix

Work through these four checks in order. Each one can end the request with a cheaper and safer answer than a new reward.

## Step 1: Find Everyone the Request Names

Navigate to **User Support** > **Person Search** and look up every email address in the request. A referral involves two people, so look up both the advocate and the friend even when the request names only one of them.

Open each profile. To see whether Extole has connected the two people, open **View Network** on the profile — it shows the relationships recorded for that person.

<Tip>
Confirm which profile you're on before you do anything else. Advocate and friend are easy to mix up, and the reward a request is really about often sits on the other one.
</Tip>

## Step 2: Check Whether the Reward Already Exists

Review the **Rewards** section of each profile, and for every reward you find, work out whether it's the one being asked about.

- **Does it belong to this referral?** Compare the related person on the reward against the other person named in the request. The two records often differ slightly — a nickname, a different email domain, a typo in an account ID — so a near match is usually the same person rather than a separate referral.
- **Was it already delivered?** The referral has been rewarded. Tell the requester so, and don't issue a second reward.
- **Did it fail, or is it still pending?** Approve the existing reward instead of creating a new one. See [How to Approve Failed Rewards](/guides/flow-campaigns/how-to-approve-failed-rewards).
- **Did it go to the wrong address?** That's a delivery problem, not a missing reward. See [Investigating Missing Rewards and Attribution](/guides/rewards-management/reward-fulfillment/how-to-investigate-a-wismr-request) for how to resend or recover it.

Ruling out an existing reward because its date or amount doesn't match the request is the most common way one referral ends up paid twice. Match on the referral between the two people first, then explain any mismatch.

## Step 3: Look for the Event That Should Have Rewarded Them

If no reward exists, find out whether the qualifying event does. Open **Show Steps** on the profile and look for the event your campaign rewards — a conversion, a registration, an account opening, a funded account.

- **The event is there and pending.** Approve it and your campaign issues the reward itself. Go to **User Support** > **Approvals**, click **Approve**, and record your own support ticket number in the note so the decision can be traced later.
- **The event is there and failed or was declined.** The approval window offers a **Force approve** checkbox for events that are low quality, declined, or still evaluating. A note is required, and **Confirm** stays disabled until you've entered one and ticked the box. Force approving overrides the quality result, so use it only when you've satisfied yourself the event is genuine.
- **The event is there but has no Program, Campaign, or Journey.** Extole received the event but couldn't attach it to a referral journey, so no reward rule ever evaluated it. Follow [Investigating Missing Rewards and Attribution](/guides/rewards-management/reward-fulfillment/how-to-investigate-a-wismr-request).
- **The event isn't there at all.** Confirm in your own systems that the action really happened, then create it. Prefer your campaign's makegood event, which issues the reward while keeping the compensation out of your organic funnel data. See [How to Manually Create a Referral or other Event](/guides/rewards-management/reward-fulfillment/how-to-manually-create-a-referral-or-other-event).

## Step 4: Confirm Before You Issue

Take your findings back to whoever raised the request and get their agreement before you create anything. Manual rewards are hard to unwind once the reward provider has delivered them.

If the request came from one of your own customers and your findings don't support a reward — the qualifying action never happened, or the referral was already rewarded — explain the outcome rather than issuing one anyway.

## Quick Reference

| What you found | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A delivered reward for this referral | Nothing. Tell the requester the referral was already rewarded. |
| A failed or pending reward | [Approve the existing reward](/guides/flow-campaigns/how-to-approve-failed-rewards). |
| A delivered reward at the wrong address | Resend or recover it. Don't create a second one. |
| No reward, and a pending event | Approve the event in **Approvals**. |
| No reward, and a failed or declined event | Approve the event, using **Force approve** if you accept the quality result. |
| An event with no Program, Campaign, or Journey | [Investigate attribution](/guides/rewards-management/reward-fulfillment/how-to-investigate-a-wismr-request). |
| No event, but you can confirm the action happened | [Create the event](/guides/rewards-management/reward-fulfillment/how-to-manually-create-a-referral-or-other-event), preferring a makegood event. |
| None of the above, and the person should still be rewarded | Issue a manual reward, below. |

# How to Issue a Manual Reward

## Step 1: Open the Participant's Profile

Navigate to **User Support** > **Person Search**, find the person, and open their profile. Scroll to the **Rewards** section and click **+ New Reward**.

<Frame caption="The Rewards section of a participant's profile, with the New Reward button.">
  <img src="/images/extole/manual-reward-new-reward-button.png" alt="Participant profile showing the Rewards section and the New Reward button" />
</Frame>

## Step 2: Select the Program, Campaign, and Reward

The **New Reward** panel opens with the person's name and email at the top. Choose the **Program**, then the **Campaign**, then the **Reward**. Each selection narrows the next.

Once a reward is selected, a link to its reward supplier appears beneath the field. Open it to confirm you're about to issue the reward you meant to.

Two things keep a reward out of reach here:

- **The campaign isn't in the list.** Only live, paused, and ended campaigns appear. A campaign that was never launched can't be selected.
- **The reward is greyed out.** The reward is turned off, or it has no reward supplier set. Hover it to see which.

## Step 3: Explain Why You're Issuing It

In the **Note** field, record why this reward is being issued manually, including your own support ticket number. The note is saved with the reward.

Example: `Manual reward for ticket 48213. Original event missing first and last name. Eligibility verified.`

## Step 4: Add the Related Person

For a two-person program, enter the other person in the referral in **Related Person** — the friend when you're rewarding the advocate, and the advocate when you're rewarding the friend. Extole identifies them by whichever identifier your account is keyed on, usually their email address.

Filling this in is what stops the same referral being rewarded twice later, and it's what makes the reward report correctly as part of that referral.

## Step 5: Set the Optional Controls

The panel shows further controls depending on how the campaign and the reward are configured:

- **Create a relationship with this person**: records a referral relationship between the two people where none exists yet.
- **Record the business event that triggered this reward in dashboard data**: includes the compensating event in your dashboard figures. Leave it off to keep a makegood out of your organic funnel data.
- **Reward Date**: the date the reward was earned, when it differs from today. It applies only to a force approved reward, and it can't be in the future.
- **Force approve this reward regardless of rule results**: issues the reward even when the campaign's rules fail. Leave it off to have Extole evaluate the rules and issue the reward only if they all pass — which is what you want when you still intend to honor reward caps and quality rules.

## Step 6: Add Any Data the Reward Needs

Some rewards need extra data to be fulfilled or to report correctly, such as an order ID, an account number, or a shipping address. Add it in **Additional Data** as key and value pairs.

| Key | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `order_id` | `12345678` |
| `account_id` | `A-4417` |

<Tip>
Open the campaign in Flow Builder and read the step that issues this reward. Its **Data** section lists exactly what the reward expects — matching it means the reward issues cleanly and appears in reporting the same way an automatic one does.
</Tip>

<Frame caption="A Flow Builder step, showing the data the reward expects.">
  <img src="/images/extole/manual-reward-flow-step-data.png" alt="Flow Builder step card showing quality rules, reward actions, and the data the step carries" />
</Frame>

## Step 7: Create the Reward

Review every field, then click **Create**.

If any of the data the reward expects is still empty, a **Missing data** confirmation lists what's absent. Go back and fill it in unless you're certain the reward doesn't need it, then click **Create Anyway**.

The reward appears in the **Rewards** section of the profile and is issued if everything it needs is in place.

# Best Practices

- **Investigate first.** Run the four checks above before creating anything.
- **Fill in everything you know.** Related person and additional data are what make a manual reward deliver reliably and report accurately.
- **Match your automatic rewards.** A manual reward should look in reporting like one the campaign issued itself.
- **Write a note someone else can use.** Include the ticket number and what you verified.
- **Reward both people separately.** To reward the advocate and the friend for the same referral, repeat the process on each profile.

# Troubleshooting

If a manual reward fails to create or fails to deliver, check that:

- Your latest campaign changes have been published.
- The reward supplier has funds, or coupon inventory, available.
- The campaign is live, paused, or ended rather than never launched.
- The reward is turned on and has a reward supplier set.

If the reward was created and then got stuck, open it from the profile and use **Re-evaluate** after correcting the underlying problem. If it stays stuck, contact Extole Support at [support@extole.com](mailto:support@extole.com) with the reward and what you've already checked.
