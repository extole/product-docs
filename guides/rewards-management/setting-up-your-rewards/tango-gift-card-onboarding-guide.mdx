# Overview

This guide prepares you to launch gift card rewards through Extole's built-in Tango integration. Use it as a checklist before your program goes live: what Extole handles for you, which gift cards to offer, the branding your creative needs, and what goes into the reward email.

Each section summarizes one step and links to the page with the full detail.

## What Extole Handles for You

You don't need your own account with Tango, and there is no separate onboarding with them. Extole is the account holder:

- **Funding**: Extole invoices you to fund your gift card balance. Credit card funding is also available.
- **Ordering and delivery**: when a participant earns a reward, Extole orders the gift card from Tango, and Tango emails it to the participant.
- **Reward email template**: your CSM builds the email template in Tango from the content you provide.

Your part is choosing the cards, preparing your creative and terms, and sending your CSM the email content.

# Before You Launch

| Step | What you do | Who else is involved |
|---|---|---|
| Choose your gift cards | Pick the cards and values in My Extole | — |
| Fund your balance | Pay the Extole invoice | Extole sends the invoice |
| Add branding requirements | Add the gift card disclaimer and "*Restrictions Apply. See Terms." to your creative, terms, and emails | — |
| Customize the reward email | Send your header image, sender name, message, and closing | Your CSM sets up the template |
| Add the reward to your campaign | Attach the reward and its Tango email delivery | Your CSM, for a legacy campaign |
| Set up alerts | Set low-balance and spending alerts | — |

## Choose Your Gift Cards

The gift cards you can offer come from Tango's live catalog. Choose a card that fits your brand and your audience, such as a retailer card or a Visa or Mastercard prepaid card.

1. Navigate to **Rewards Center** and click **+ New Reward**.
2. Select **US Gift Card** or **International Gift Card**.
3. Choose the gift card and its value.

Create one reward for each card and value you want to offer. To let participants pick from many retailers with one reward, see [Offer Advocates Multiple Gift Card Options](/guides/rewards-management/setting-up-your-rewards/offer-advocates-multiple-gift-card-options).

Full steps: [How to Reward with Tango Gift Cards](/guides/rewards-management/setting-up-your-rewards/how-to-reward-with-tango-gift-cards).

## Fund Your Gift Card Balance

Your gift card rewards all draw on one gift card account. My Extole creates it the first time you create a gift card reward, with a balance of zero, and no gift cards are sent until the balance is funded.

Each gift card issued is deducted from your balance. When your account reaches its low-balance amount, Extole sends an invoice to replenish it. Plan your first funding around the number of rewards you expect in your launch period.

# Gift Card Creative and Branding

If you name or show a branded gift card in your program, each card's brand sets its own requirements. The common requirements are:

- Your landing page includes the gift card disclaimer.
- Your terms and conditions include a gift card disclaimer section.
- The header of your share experience and mobile landing page includes "*Restrictions Apply. See Terms."
- Every email you send to participants includes the gift card disclaimer.

If you use the gift card's brand name in a call-to-action or a promotional email, include the gift card's terms and conditions as well.

To find the disclaimer and branding requirements for a card:

1. Navigate to **Rewards Center** > **Gift Cards**.
2. Click the card.
3. Click **See Brand Overview**.

Collect these for every card you offer before you design your creative, so your designer and legal team work from the brand's own wording.

# Customize the Tango Reward Email

Tango sends the reward email, not Extole, so the template is set up by your CSM in Tango. Send your CSM these elements:

| Element | What you provide | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| **Header image** | Your branded header | 650 x 150 px, JPEG or PNG |
| **Sender name** | The name the email comes from, for example "\{Company Name\} Referral Rewards" | The address is always **noreply@tangocard.com** |
| **Message** | The copy at the top of the email | Up to 3,000 characters; simple HTML styling is allowed |
| **Closing** | The copy at the end of the email | Add a link to your referral program so recipients can keep referring |

The redemption text and instructions are not editable. Tango adds them automatically based on the gift card.

Full detail: [Tango Reward Email](/guides/programs-and-campaigns/tango-reward-email).

# Add the Reward to Your Campaign

- **Flow Builder campaign**: attach the gift card reward and replace the default reward email with a **Tango Email** delivery. See [How to Set Up a Tango Gift Card Reward](/guides/flow-campaigns/how-to-set-up-a-tango-gift-card-reward).
- **Legacy campaign**: swap the reward in the campaign's **Overview** or **Rules** tab. The first time you add a Tango reward, your CSM enables the Gift Card Configuration zone with your Tango template ID. See [How to Reward with Tango Gift Cards](/guides/rewards-management/setting-up-your-rewards/how-to-reward-with-tango-gift-cards#adding-the-card-to-a-campaign).

Update your campaign copy so it names the reward and its value, then publish your changes.

## Set Up Balance and Spending Alerts

Navigate to **Rewards Center** > **Gift Cards** and click **Edit Spending Limits**. Set an alert for a low balance and for the reward value spent in an hour or a day, so you hear about a funding need or unusual spend before it affects your program.

# What Recipients Experience

1. The participant earns the reward. Gift cards are sent within minutes of qualifying; if you approve rewards after a waiting period, such as a return window, the card is sent after approval.
2. Tango emails the participant from **noreply@tangocard.com**, with a link to redeem the card.
3. For a Visa or Mastercard prepaid card, the participant fills out a form on the redemption portal and creates an account. They can request a physical card for a \$3 fee taken from the card's value.
4. For an Amazon gift card, the participant enters the claim code in their Amazon account.

Expiration depends on the card. Check each card's brand overview, and state it in your terms.

If a participant reports a missing email, ask them to check their spam folder and any filters for **noreply@tangocard.com**. To resend a reward, see [How to Resend Tango Rewards](/guides/rewards-management/reward-fulfillment/how-to-resend-tango-rewards).
