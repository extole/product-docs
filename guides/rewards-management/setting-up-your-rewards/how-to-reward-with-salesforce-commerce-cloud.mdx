# Overview

You can use the Extole Salesforce Commerce Cloud Cartridge as a reward option. This integration allows you to automatically replenish coupons without the need to upload coupon codes into My Extole.

# Connect Salesforce Commerce Cloud to Extole

The first step of enabling this reward option is to [install and configure the Extole Cartridge](https://www.salesforce.com/products/commerce-cloud/partner-marketplace/partners/extole/). You can follow our guide on this process here: [Salesforce Commerce Cloud (SFRA).](https://docs.extole.com/docs/salesforce-commerce-cloud-sfra)

# Set Up Your Integration in My Extole

1. In My Extole, go to **Partners** and open the [Salesforce](https://my.extole.com/partners/salesforce) integration.
2. Click **+ Add Account**.
3. Enter your SFCC account information: **Name**, **Base URI**, **Site ID**, **OCAPI Client Id (Username)** and **OCAPI Password**.
4. Click **Save**.

Once your account is added, click **Manage Coupons** to open the **SFCC Coupons** page, where you set up your coupons.

# Configure SFCC Coupons in My Extole

Go to **Rewards** > [**SFCC Coupons**](https://my.extole.com/rewards-center/display-type/SALESFORCE_COUPON). The page lists your SFCC coupon sets.

To add a coupon set:

1. Click **+ New Coupon** in the top right corner.
2. In **Select Reward**, choose the Salesforce Commerce Cloud reward, listed as `SALESFORCE_COUPON`. Enter a name and click **Add**. My Extole copies the SFCC reward your account already uses. The option appears only when a campaign in your account already uses an SFCC reward.
3. Open the new coupon set and click **Edit**.
4. Fill in the fields below and click **Save**.

- **Salesforce Account**: The SFCC account you added under **Partners**.
- **Salesforce Commerce Cloud Coupon Set**: The coupon set in your SFCC account. Extole lists all sets associated with the SFCC account you set up in your integration.
- **Name**: The name of the coupon set in My Extole.
- **Value**: The value of the coupon codes, and its type: USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, **Percent Off** or **Points**.
- **Refill Amount**: This is the number of coupon codes you will want automatically refilled when you hit a minimum threshold.

# Viewing Your Reward and Troubleshooting

Once your reward is created, it appears in the [Rewards Center](https://my.extole.com/rewards-center) list. Click either the name of the coupon set or its type.

**If you select the reward type (Salesforce Commerce Cloud)**

This will take you to the reward type and give you a high-level overview of all of your Salesforce Commerce Cloud (SFCC) coupon codes.

The table shows each coupon set's **Name**, **Value / Type**, the number of coupons **Remaining** and **Used**, and when it was **Last Updated**.

**If you select the actual reward (your coupon set)**

This will take you to the coupon set's details: its **Reward value**, **Coupon name**, **Reward balance** and **Refill threshold**. Under **Reward Activity Stream**, it lists each request Extole has made to Salesforce for more codes, with its **Operation Type**, **Offset**, **Number Requested**, **Date** and **Status**. This is extremely important when troubleshooting the reward activities and ensuring that everything is hooked up properly.

To refill the coupon set now, click **Refill Manually**. Extole requests more codes from Salesforce right away.
