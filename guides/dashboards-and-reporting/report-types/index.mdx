---
title: "Report Types"
---

- [Recommended Reports](/guides/dashboards-and-reporting/recommended-reports) — the reports most clients start with.
- [Audience Reports](/guides/dashboards-and-reporting/report-types/audience-reports/index) — lists of people and audience counts.
- [Rewards Reports](/guides/dashboards-and-reporting/report-types/rewards-reports/nominations-report) — earned, fulfilled, and failed rewards.

To build a report none of these cover, define your own columns with [mapping expressions](/guides/dashboards-and-reporting/configuring-reports/custom-data-queries-using-extole-reports) — start from [Configurable Report Types and Their Entities](/guides/dashboards-and-reporting/configuring-reports/configurable-report-types-and-their-entities).
