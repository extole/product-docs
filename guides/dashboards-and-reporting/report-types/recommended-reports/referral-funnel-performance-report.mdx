# Overview

The Referral Funnel Performance report gives a broad overview of your referral funnel, with optional benchmarking. Use it to analyze any combination of people counts, event counts, and formulas.

The report has two main sections: **Filters** and **Advanced**. First, some terminology: **steps** are the events in your program, and an event typically has an actor (a friend or an advocate) and an action.

## Filters

Filters are what you use to distill your data.

- **Date Range:** The range of data in the report. These are trailing date and time ranges, so "last week" is the last seven days.
- **Period:** How the data is grouped — by day, week, month, or year.
- **Program Label:** By default, the report pulls data from all events in all programs. You can also analyze one program at a time.
- **Campaign Id:** By default, the report pulls data from all events in all campaigns. You can also analyze one campaign at a time.
- **Source:** No default sources — the report shows events from all sources. You can show events from specific sources, such as email or a specific zone tag.
- **Channel** (under **Advanced**): No default channels — the report shows events from all channels. You can show events from specific channels, such as Email, Facebook, Twitter, WhatsApp, or Pinterest.
- **Quality** (under **Advanced**): Defaults to All. This indicates the quality of the event. You can filter by events of all quality, only high quality, or only low quality.
- **Visit Type** (under **Advanced**): Defaults to All. This indicates the type of visit the person made. Filter by people you have seen for the first time (New To Client), who visited the program for the first time (New To Program), or who visited the campaign for the first time (New To Campaign).

## Configuring the Report

Below are defaults for a typical Refer A Friend program. To count people or events beyond these, go to the **Advanced** section, select a `step_name`, and name the output column. For example, the first row below uses the `step_name` `promotion_clicked` with the column name Promotion Clickers.

### The People

In order, these give you:

- A count of people who clicked a promotion (a global header, global footer, overlay, confirmation pop-up, or mobile notification, or a link from a promotion email such as a welcome email, mail after purchase email, stats email, or reward email)
- A count of people who viewed an on-site or in-app promotion (a global header, global footer, overlay, confirmation pop-up, or mobile notification)
- A count of people who advocated on your behalf (shared, referred, rated, or reviewed)
- A count of people who clicked a share
- A count of people who registered
- A count of people who converted

Attribution type refers to whether you want to see referral-related people, people who are not referral-related, or all people.

<Info>
Reward emails go to both advocates and friends. Because they contain calls-to-action, they count as promotion emails and promotion experiences. If your program uses email capture, it counts in the `registered` event.
</Info>

### The Events

Use the Events section to get a count of events, such as shares. To count people or events beyond the suggested defaults, select a `step_name` and name the output column.

Attribution type refers to whether you want to see referral-related events, events that are not referral-related, or all events.

### The Formulas

Using the column names defined in the People and Event sections, you can create formulas that define relationships between those columns. Your columns must be defined first — in the People Count or Event Count section — before you can use them in a formula. You have two options: percentages and ratios.

For example, Advocate Click Through Rate = (Promotion Clickers ÷ Impression Viewers) × 100.

To track progress toward a goal, toggle on **Include Goal**, enter a numerical goal, and name the column that tracks your progress.

## Report Output

In the output, you see the information you specified with the filters:

- **Period Start and End Time:** The start and end time of a given period
- **Period:** How the data is grouped
- **Program Label:** The label of the program for the corresponding data
- **Campaign Id:** The id of the campaign for the corresponding data

You also see the column names you specified in the People Count, Event Count, and Formulas sections.

For a formula that includes a goal, the goal shows as [Column Name] Benchmark. Using the example above, the output columns are:

| Advocate Click Through Rate | CTR Percent to Goal | Advocate Click Through Rate Benchmark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 37 | 74 | 50 |

The output also includes a Total row that sums your data. To exclude totals, toggle off **Include Totals** in the **Advanced** section.

The **Advanced** section is also where you set your dimensions. The default dimensions group by Program Label and Campaign Id, but you can also group by dimensions such as Language or Country.
