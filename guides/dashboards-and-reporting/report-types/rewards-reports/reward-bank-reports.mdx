# Overview

Three reports cover Reward Bank activity. Together they answer what members have earned, what they
have spent, and what happened to any individual reward.

Run them from the [Reports](https://my.extole.com/reports) page in My Extole. For how to run and
schedule a report, see [How to Run a Report](/guides/dashboards-and-reporting/how-to-run-a-report).

# The Three Reward Bank Reports

## Reward Bank Rewards Summary

An overview of total reward activity, summarizing key metrics and redemption trends.

Use this as the regular health check on a loyalty program. It is the report to look at first when
you want to know whether earning and redemption are in balance.

## Reward Bank Redeemed Rewards

The rewards that have been redeemed, along with the associated Collectible Rewards.

Use this to see what members are actually choosing. Because it ties each redemption back to the
Collectible Rewards spent, it also shows how long members hold a balance before spending it.

## Reward Bank Rewards Audit

A detailed log of all Collectible Reward Bank rewards, including their current statuses and
history.

Use this when you need the record for a specific reward or member — a support investigation, a
disputed balance, or a reconciliation against your own systems.

# How This Reporting Can Help You

* See which ways to earn are actually driving participation, and which are issuing points nobody values.
* Compare points issued against points redeemed, and watch the gap between them.
* Investigate a member's balance when they contact support.
* Reconcile reward activity against your own systems.

# Related Reward Reports

Reward Bank reports cover the bank itself. These standard reward reports cover the rewards that
flow through it, and accept a reward supplier filter so you can narrow them to loyalty:

| Report | Shows |
| :--- | :--- |
| [Earned Rewards](/guides/dashboards-and-reporting/report-types/rewards-reports/earned-rewards-report) | Participants who earned rewards, with reward status, type, and face value. Face value type is `POINTS` for point-denominated rewards. |
| [Reward Transitions](/guides/dashboards-and-reporting/report-types/rewards-reports/reward-transitions-report) | Rewards that changed state in a period — earned to fulfilled, fulfilled to redeemed. The closest thing to a movement log. |
| [Failed Rewards](/guides/dashboards-and-reporting/report-types/rewards-reports/failed-rewards-report) | Rewards that did not issue, and why. Use when members report missing points. |
| [Fulfilled Rewards](/guides/dashboards-and-reporting/report-types/recommended-reports/fulfilled-rewards-report) | Rewards delivered to participants. |

# Working Out Earned, Redeemed, and Outstanding

The two numbers a loyalty program is judged on are how much has been issued and how much has been
spent. Derive them as follows:

**Earned**: total points issued. Run **Earned Rewards** filtered to your Collectible Reward
suppliers, and sum face value.

**Redeemed**: total value spent. Run **Reward Bank Redeemed Rewards**, or **Reward Transitions**
filtered to the transition into a redeemed state.

**Outstanding**: earned minus redeemed, less anything expired or revoked. This is the balance still
sitting with members.

<Warning>
  Outstanding points are a commitment you have made and not yet paid for. Track the figure on a
  schedule from the start of the program rather than calculating it for the first time when
  somebody asks. A balance that only grows means members have not found a reason to spend.
</Warning>
