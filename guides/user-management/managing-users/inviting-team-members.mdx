# Overview

Invite team members from **Settings** so they can register and sign in to My Extole.

# Invite a Team Member

1. In the left navigation, click **Settings**. You can also open [Settings](https://my.extole.com/settings) directly.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/extole/inviting-team-members-settings-nav.png"
    alt="The Settings item in the My Extole left navigation."
  />
</Frame>

2. In the **Team** section, click **+ New Team Member**. The **Invite Team Member** drawer opens.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/extole/inviting-team-members-team.png"
    alt="The Team section on the Settings page, with the + New Team Member button."
  />
</Frame>

3. Enter their email address.

4. Under **Permissions**, leave **User Support** unselected to give them full Admin access. Select **User Support** to limit them to the User Support pages of My Extole. For what each role can do, see [User Roles & Permissions](/guides/user-management/managing-users/user-roles-permissions).

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/extole/inviting-team-members-drawer.png"
    alt="The Invite Team Member drawer with an email field and a User Support permission option."
  />
</Frame>

5. Click **Invite**.

6. They follow the link in the invitation email and set a password before they can sign in.

# If the Invitation Link Expires

An invitation link is valid for 7 days after it is sent, and it can be used only once. Someone who follows a link that has expired, or that has already been used, sees an expired-link message instead of the registration page.

To get them a working link, open the **Team** section of [Settings](https://my.extole.com/settings) and resend their invite email, as described in [Managing Your Extole Users](/guides/user-management/managing-users/managing-your-extole-users). For the other reasons a link can stop working, see [Login Troubleshooting Tips](/guides/notifications-and-troubleshooting/platform-and-admin/login-troubleshooting-tips).
